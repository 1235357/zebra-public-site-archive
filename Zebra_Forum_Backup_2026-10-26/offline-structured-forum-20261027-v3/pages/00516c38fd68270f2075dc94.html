All, Do any of your customers deploy any kind of accountability / user tracking software on their devices? By which I mean, a user picks up a device and has to sign on before they can do anything (pretty standard stuff), but then that sign on potentially will define what applications they have access to, and also at a corporate level they can track who used which device and when such that if it goes missing or gets damaged they can work out who is responsible. It seems quite simple but I can't think of many customers doing more than a simple sign-on and not taking it to the next step. This is primarily for a retail store environment but any large scale deployments would be good. Thanks IJ

I don't have answers persay to this, but some experience and suggestions...&nbsp; I came from a warehouse and we came up with a database that the user had to scan their employee badge and a barcode on the mobile computer to sign it out, or the unit was permanently assigned to them.&nbsp; It has flaws as it's a very manual process. This company: http://www.opticalphusion.com/&nbsp;is a partner of ours and they were recently in our office to talk about their solutions and we got on this dicussion.&nbsp; They are doing some things with RFID to track the unit, some of our new mobile computers come with an RFID tag in them already, and then they put an RFID tag on the employees badge, with reader to track who has what.&nbsp; I have a contact if you would like it... As far as signing on and selectiong various apps, what about App Center?&nbsp; I think that can do what you are looking for.&nbsp; The other option or an additional option is to create a group or sign on to the Wireless Network, so the user would need to sign on to even gain access to the network, you can normally track a MAC address and association at that point... Hope this helps... Jesse
