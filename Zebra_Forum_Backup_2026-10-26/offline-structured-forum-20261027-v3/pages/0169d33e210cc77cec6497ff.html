Hello!

I'm working on an android application for the RFC40 scanning device, and been following the documentation for enabling RFID and Barcode scanning. I've had success enabling RFID scanning but when I attempt to switch to barcode scanning, I don't seem to be any events triggered when I scan a barcode.

 

I've essentially followed the documentation here -> https://techdocs.zebra.com/dcs/rfid/android/2-0-5-226/samples/fx_hellorfid/

 

the only difference I have is I have invoked reader.Config.setTriggerMode(BARCODE_MODE, true); after device connection.

The barcode scanner appears to work and beeps when I scan a barcode, but there are no events triggered in the RfidEventsListener and therefore I don't receive the scanned barcode in my project. Where could I be going wrong?

Datawedge is one option (Which Uses the Barcode Scanner on the Zebra Android Device.)
If you do not have Zebra Android - and use the RFD40_P+ or the RFD90 - or the RFD8500i the that uses the DCS Scanner Control libraries bundled within the RFID SDK for Android Studio.

123RFID Mobile app - is an excellent example of how that works/ functions for RFD40 / RFD90 specifically. The HHSampleApp does work to (for basics), but 123RFID Mobile (Android - or iOS) - shows all the features.
