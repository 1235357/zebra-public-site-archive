Just realized that the ZQ220 model is not a Link-OS mode. The ZebraDesigner for Developers application connects to the printers through the ZebraDesigner Windows Driver v8.x, which supports the Link-OS printers only. Therefore, you cannot use the ZebraDesigner for Developers with the ZQ220 printers. In addition, ZQ220 doesn't support ZPL, while the ZebraDesigner for Developers doesn't support non-ZPL format language. That's the reason why the ZQ220 is not showing up in the ZebraDesigner for Developers.
