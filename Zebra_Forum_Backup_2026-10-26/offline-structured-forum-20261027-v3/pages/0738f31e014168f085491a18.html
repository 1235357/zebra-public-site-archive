I have not heard the issue like this. How do you achieve the batch print? Do you send the same identical ZPL to the printer to print identical copies of the label? Or do you send the ZPL with different text in ^FB commands for each label? For printing identical label multiple times, we could use ^PQ (Print Quantity) command inside the ZPL, rather than sending the identical ZPL multiple times.
