Hi Scott,

Try this option:

BXN --&gt; Normal orientation, or BXI --&gt; inverted orientation. I used Zebra Designer Tool to generate the right ZPL code. If you want to do the same, please, donwload a free version from the link below, but you should choose the driver with extension ZPL name.

ZebraDesigner Basic Barcode Label Software | Zebra

For my test I used a ZD620 desktop printer with driver in ZPL.

^XA
^MMT
^PW609
^LL0406
^LS0
^BY60,60^FT259,158^BXN,5,200,0,0,1,~
^FH\^FD01010101^FS
^FT162,69^A0N,28,28^FH\^FDTest with leading Zero^FS
^PQ1,0,1,Y^XZ

Please, let me know if this worked.

Thanks,
MC
