Hello Zebra Developer Support,

We have developed a Flutter iOS application that communicates with Zebra ZQ511 mobile printers over Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

Our iOS application uses Zebra's Bluetooth MFi communication and therefore includes the following protocol:

com.zebra.rawport

Apple App Review has blocked our application because the app has not been authorized by the accessory manufacturer to work with the MFi accessory.

Apple has instructed us to contact Zebra and request that our application be added to the MFi Product Plan.

Please let us know what information you require from us to authorize the application.

Apple has also requested the MFi Product Plan ID (PPID) to be provided in App Store Connect.

App Name: Stopdeck Driver

Bundle ID: com.oneKindExpress.Driver

Zebra Printer: ZQ511

Platform: iOS / Flutter

Protocol: com.zebra.rawport

Please provide the applicable MFi Product Plan ID/PPID and confirm once the application has been added to the MFi Product Plan.

Thank you.
