Hi Alex,

We have a few options for printing PDFs on our printers.

Unofficially (i.e. not supported by Zebra), there is a print service available on Google Play which is essentially an Android Print service designed for our printers. This is available here: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zebra.zebraprintservi… & source code is also public here: https://github.com/Thunderlight-Software/Zebra-Print-Service

Alternatively, if your printer has PDF Direct installed you should be able to use our SDK to send the PDF file accross: https://www.zebra.com/gb/en/products/software/barcode-printers/link-os/…. PDF Direct is Free of charge when running Link-OS 6.3 or later.

Thanks,

James
