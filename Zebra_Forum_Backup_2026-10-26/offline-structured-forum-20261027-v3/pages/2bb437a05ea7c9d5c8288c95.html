Fonts are generally copyrighted artworks and usually license is required&nbsp;for use. Zebra printers have a number of pre-installed fonts, which are named as Zebra 0, A, B, … etc. You will have to find which pre-installed font is close to Helvetica font you want. If none of the pre-installed font meets your needs, then you will have to source your own font&nbsp;and install the font&nbsp;to the printer. The Printer Setup Utility can help you to download your font to a printer.
Additional&nbsp;fonts (Swiss 721 font and EPL Asian font)&nbsp;can be downloaded from the Printer Fonts page on www.zebra.com.
Hope this helps
