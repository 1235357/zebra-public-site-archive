Hi, when you export the DataWedge configuration it should tell you where the configuration was exported to, you can then use adb to `adb pull ` the files from the device.&nbsp; You can browse into dedicated 'Zebra Folders' using the Files app on your device, which will be /data/tmp/public and /enterprise/usr.&nbsp;For Chrome browser, there is no way to 'copy' the configuration off of the device.
