O
Ondrej Kincl
2 months 1 week ago
331 1
//Expert user has replied.

I am implementing an Android application that connects a phone with Zebra DS2278 Scanner using SSI Bluetooth Low Energy. 

I can generate a barcode using dcssdkGetPairingBarcode function. Scanning this barcode pairs and connects the scanner. Great. But when I disconnect the scanner using dcssdkTerminateCommunicationSession, I cannot undo this with dcssdkEstablishCommunicationSession (it returns error 2: DCSSDK_RESULT_SCANNER_NOT_AVAILABLE). That's strange. I would expect this to swiftly reconnect the scanner (which is already paired). 

My second problem is that when I close the app and reopen, the SDK fails to find the scanner and I cannot reconnect the device without generating the barcode and pairing again. Needless to say, pairing anew on every app launch with barcode is too clumsy to be used in production.

I already spent many hours trying to find a solution with no avail, so I appreciate any help. 

DS 2278 Scanner
Google Pixel 9a
Android 16
Zebra Scanner SDK for Android 2.7.3.0
Building with macOS 26.5 in Android Studio Quail 1

The first behavior is unfortunately expected since you initiated a disconnect. You can do double trigger on DS2278 to initiate auto reconnect from the scanner .

For second behavior , there is auto reconnect feature you can enable in SDK . 

please check Scanner SDK for Android - About - Zebra Technologies
