The 7627 chipset uses the Qualcomm gpsOne Gen 7 engine.&nbsp; This provides -160dBm standalone sensitivity (so called tracking dBm). You can reference Qualcomm doc 80-VU455-1 B for this information if needed. Unfortunately due to the license agreement with Qualcomm, this actual document cannot be shared.
