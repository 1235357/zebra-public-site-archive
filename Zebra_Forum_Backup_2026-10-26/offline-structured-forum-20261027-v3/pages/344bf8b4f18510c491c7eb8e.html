IESample in CE5 is based on IE6.&nbsp; IE6 supports this registry key to disable autocomplete.&nbsp; I don't have the means to test whether or not this functionality from Big Windows IE6 made it into CE5 IESample.
[HKEY_CURRENT_USER \Software \Microsoft \Windows \CurrentVersion \Explorer\AutoComplete]

AutoSuggest = "No"
