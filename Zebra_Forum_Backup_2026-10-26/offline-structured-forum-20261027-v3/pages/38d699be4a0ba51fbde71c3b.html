Hi Seb
I hope you have downloaded the Postal sample app.

To configure SimulScan,

Copy the relevant SimulScan templates to /enterprise/device/settings/datawedge/templates folder.
Open DataWedge
Click on Profile0
Go to SimulScan plug-in and enable it.
Select the Postal template under Templates option.
Scroll to KeyStroke plug-in and disable it.
Scroll to Intent plug-in and set the intent action as "com.example.datawedge.postal"
Set the intent category as "android.intent.category.DEFAULT"
Close DataWedge and Open the sample app.
Press the trigger to start SimulScan ViewFinder
Scan a Postal form.

You should be able to see the data appear in the sample app.

If you have any questions please let me know.
Tharindu.
