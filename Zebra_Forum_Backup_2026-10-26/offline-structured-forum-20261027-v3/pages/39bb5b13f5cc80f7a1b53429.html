All Zebra Link-OS printers support Weblink/WebSocket. Only a few legacy printer models and Link-OS Basic printers do not support Weblink/WebSocket, such as, the ZD220, ZD230, ZT111, etc.

Yes, we have customers build their own servers and connect Zebra printers over the Weblink/WebSocket successfully, though we cannot name them here. To get started, you can use the Link-OS® Multiplatform SDK for Webservice to build your own Tomcat 7/8 based webserver and connect with any Zebra Link-OS printers straight forward. The SDK package has all the information and documentations on how to get started.
