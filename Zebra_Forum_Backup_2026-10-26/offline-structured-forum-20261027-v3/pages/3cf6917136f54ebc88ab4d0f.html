Not sure why EHS would be preventing the intents from being sent/received but DWDemo uses the same intent method to receive barcode data so you could test it by adding it to enterprisehomescreen.xml as follows:

<application label="DWDemo" package="com.symbol.datawedge" activity="com.symbol.datawedge.DWDemoActivity"/>

If it works then I think the issue must be related to your app code in some way
