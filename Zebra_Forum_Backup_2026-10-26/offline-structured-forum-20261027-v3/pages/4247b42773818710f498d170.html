Unfortunately, the list of supported printers pointed to by the quoted link is not up to date. The list should have included all Link-OS printers. ZQ610 is Link-OS and supported by the Browser Print. All Link-OS printers are supported by the Browser Print.

Zebra Print DNA is an umbrella term that includes printer product categories, tools, software and SDKs, etc.
