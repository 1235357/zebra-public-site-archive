Are you using the Browser Print SDK on Android and try to discover the printer over Wi-Fi? Can you try if you can find the printer through the demo webapp - https://cagdemo.com/ from your Android device?

Make sure that the Wi-Fi network doesn't block the multicast packets, as the Browser Print SDK utilize the multicast to discover the printer on the network.
