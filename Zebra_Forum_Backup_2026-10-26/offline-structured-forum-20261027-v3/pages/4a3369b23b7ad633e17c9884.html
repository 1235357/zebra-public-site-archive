Hello Zebra team,

We are stuck in the iOS MFi whitelisting process and need help reaching someone who can move it forward.

Timeline:
- May 31: Apple rejected our app (guideline 2.1.0) because the com.zebra.rawport protocol is declared but not yet authorized by Zebra. Apple confirmed no new binary is needed - only the PPID.
- July 4: We emailed iOSApp@zebra.com and received a prompt reply from a Zebra engineer requesting Appendix A, TestFlight access, and a test account.
- July 11: We sent everything requested: complete Appendix A, a TestFlight external-tester invite to the Apple ID your engineer provided, the public TestFlight link, test account credentials, and step-by-step printing instructions.
- July 17 and July 21: Follow-up emails. No response to either.
- As of today, the TestFlight invitation has never been accepted, so the review has not started.

App details:
- App: Osool: Pocket Supply Chain (com.osool.app), procurement/inventory platform
- Protocol: com.zebra.rawport
- Printers: ZQ521 (primary), ZQ511
- Distribution: Standard App Store, existing app
- Region: Saudi Arabia (EMEA)

Our App Store release has now been blocked for almost two months on this single item. Could someone from the Zebra MFi/whitelisting team pick this up, or point us to the correct contact for the EMEA region? We can provide TestFlight access and anything else needed immediately.
