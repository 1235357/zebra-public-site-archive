Hello Nicolas,

Blinking Data and Media lights during receipt printing usually indicate a media tracking mismatch in your ZPL. Since you are using continuous receipt paper, the printer will error out if it is looking for label gaps or marks.

Please update the ZPL payload in your Angular app to include:

  1. ^MNN – Sets media tracking to Continuous.
  2. ^LL – Defines the exact receipt length (e.g., ^LL400).

For specific details on these commands, refer to the Zebra ZPL II Programming Guide.

Let us know if this resolves the issue!
