Hi Richard, 

Apologies for the long waiting time...

We'll update the OEMInfo documentation in April but in the meantime I've managed to retrieve the URI for the SIM EID acquiring process:

content://oem_info/wan/eid

Just use it in the same way like with the other ones and remember to properly give access to this new constant, either with StageNow or with the EMDK through the AccessMgr APIs.

Don't hesitate to reach out to me in case you have any further questions.

Daniel
