Since this solution is overloading the car cradle solution, you can easily monitor the "Car Dock" events provided by native Android.&nbsp;

You can look here for specific Intents to monitor: http://developer.android.com/training/monitoring-device-state/docking-m…

You will get ACTION_DOCK_EVENT each time the hall effect is triggered and released.&nbsp; The extras in the Intent tell you if the hall effect has been activated (EXTRA_DOCK_STATE_CAR ) or released (EXTRA_DOCK_STATE_UNDOCKED)
