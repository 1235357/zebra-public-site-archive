Justin,

My understanding is that the only way to upgrade the RS6000s is using one of these cradles:

CRD-NGRS-1S1BU-01
CRD-NWTRS-2SUCH-01

These two cradles will give you access to the I/O connector on the RS6000 – you will also need a&nbsp; microUSB cable from the cradle to the PC/Laptop.

The WT6000 can be upgraded using a cradle or a special USB/Charging cable (CBL-NGWT-USBCHG-01)
