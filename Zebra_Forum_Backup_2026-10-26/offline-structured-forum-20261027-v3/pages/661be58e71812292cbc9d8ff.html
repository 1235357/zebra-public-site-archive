Quick Note - there is no such device as an FXR9600 - it is the "FXR90" Fixed Reader platform.

The FX-Series - have the FX9600.   And yes I have witnessed Samsung platforms when pairing to the BT Interface - of the FXR90 where FXR90 may need Wifi shutdown and  the Cellular shutdown. I cannot confirm this is the case with other Android Platforms.
The iOS can connect to the reader without these - but this is due to the capability of iOS and the bluetooth support for same.

Please Understand that the FXR90 is best connected to "Cloud Instances where MQTT can be used".

Using BT - is more for the ability to configure the device where the USB cable connection is not optimal.
