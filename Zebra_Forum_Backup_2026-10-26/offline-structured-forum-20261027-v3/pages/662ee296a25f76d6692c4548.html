Hi,

I experience issues with the reconnect functionality.

I tested also the demo app (https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/software/scanner-software/net-maui-wrapper.html?downloadId=f2762d90-7643-476c-98ca-068dea9486cf) with the same results.

Android Device: Zebra ET65 (Android 13)

Scanner Device: DS3678 (latest firmware)

 

If the scanner (bluetooth device) is not known by the device and you scan the paring code on the tablet, the connection works perfect.

If the scanner (bluetooth device) is allready known by the device and you rescan the pairing barcode, it sometimes works.

Same for application restart: it sometimes reconnects automatically.

There is one thing consistent in all scenarios: the event 'EVENT_SCANNER_APPEARANCE' is always there, but 9 tries on 10, the connect 'failes' [using scannerManager.AutoReconnectToLastConnectedScannerOnAppRelaunch(true);].
