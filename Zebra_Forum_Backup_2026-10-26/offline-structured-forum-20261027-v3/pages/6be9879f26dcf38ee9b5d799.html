from Chi, &nbsp;tested and confirms this works.
You can use the following reg key:

&nbsp;

[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\System\GPS\TI_1283]

"StartType" = dword:0&nbsp; ;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; 0: cold start, 1:hot start,

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

&nbsp;

where default &nbsp;value is&nbsp; 1&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; in the normal OS settings.
