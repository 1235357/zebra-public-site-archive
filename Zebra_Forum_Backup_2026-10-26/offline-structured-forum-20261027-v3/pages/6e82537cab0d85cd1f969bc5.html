If the operating system detects that the object store is corrupt it will clean boot. You can test this by formatting the system volume - warmboot and it will cleanboot.&nbsp; Some ISV's use this as a method to force a cleanboot without the battery checks...(Please do not advise ISV's to do this). SPR18183 appears to be related to removing the battery - so please advise customers to power off before removing the battery to try and prevent this from happening.
