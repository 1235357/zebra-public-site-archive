I think there is more involved to turn that functionality on - but there is no Simple/Easy demo for this (Like PowerSession or 123RFID Desktop).

The ZoneID Details is in this document and there are only 3 references to it (if you search for "zoneid" in the PDF:

https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/support-dam/en/documentation/unrestricted/guide/software/interface-control-guide-en.pdf

I also do not know off the top of my Head if the "Antenna Beam Configuration" as described on PP39 on this Integrators Guide:

https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/support-dam/en/documentation/unrestricted/guide/product/atr7000-ig-en.pdf

Uses the Antenna types / "naming" convention used there for the ZoneID "Zones" 

Sorry if there is not enough detail for this here - I am aware this capability exists - I have not seen it used in a Demo App that connects to the ATR7000 Standalone.
