If the Device is being controlled via an MDM - Stagenow may not have rights to make these changes you mention.

If the device is not enrolled in an MDM - and Stagenow can configure proxy for Ethernet using:
https://techdocs.zebra.com/mx/ethernetmgr/
Or for Wifi using:
https://techdocs.zebra.com/mx/wifi/

NOTE: The MX version for the profile in StageNow, needs to be above V5 to allow these Managers to function.
