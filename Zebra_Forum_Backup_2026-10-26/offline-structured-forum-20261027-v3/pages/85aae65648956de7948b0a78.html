I have developed a Gate Management System using the FX 9600 RFID reader. The system is designed to detect vehicles with RFID stickers, verify them through the application, and open a boom barrier upon successful verification. The demo without the boom barrier has been successful.

Now, I need assistance with the following:

  1. Hardware Recommendations: What specific hardware components, including the boom barrier and sensors, should I recommend to the customer for purchase?
  2. Barrier Control Integration: How can I control the boom barrier through the application, including:
    • Sending a command to open the barrier after successful RFID verification.
    • Ensuring the barrier does not close if a vehicle is underneath it and automatically closes after the vehicle passes.

Any guidance on these points would be greatly appreciated.

Although not an RFID or Zebra-Specific issue - Access Control Systems - can have remote operation via a GPIO (GPO) that would operate a Contact Closure for activating the Barrier.

A good suggestion is to work with the Building Management / Access Control system partner to then consider methods to integrate  the RFID Use-Case solution to allow the access controls.  ( Also you can Google for these system providers.)

 

Please Note: As you can see -- I did not make any recommendation - other than the high-level partner suggestion. 
This is due to the Human-Safety considerations with Access Controls. These systems need to correctly designed / built / integrated to not pose a risk to persons - whose access provisions are being considered.
