Hi Rohan, I don't have a device here so I can't speak with certainty but I suspect this has something to do with the DataWedge profile.&nbsp; The profile used by the DWDemo application is called 'DWDemo' and you will see it in the 'DataWedge Profiles' screen if you launch DataWedge.&nbsp; I suggest you compare the profile for your application against the profile for DWDemo and you might want to start with "Configure scanner settings" (under barcode input) --&gt; "Reader params" --&gt; "Aim Type" which I suspect may need to be 'Presentation' on the CC500 but I'm not sure.
