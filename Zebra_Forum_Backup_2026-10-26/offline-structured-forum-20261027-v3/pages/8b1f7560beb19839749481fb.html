Device identifiers like Serial are otherwise obfuscated on these higher versions of Android and you as a developer are no longer able to query for them or interact with them directly. This is a problem fundamental to all Android devices, and is not just a Zebra device problem.

Zebra has however built OEMInfo to help solve this problem for enterprise developers that still rely on the true unique device identifiers like Serial and MAC. 

https://techdocs.zebra.com/oeminfo/consume/

You will however need to have permissions granted to your app to allow it to leverage and access OEMInfo.
