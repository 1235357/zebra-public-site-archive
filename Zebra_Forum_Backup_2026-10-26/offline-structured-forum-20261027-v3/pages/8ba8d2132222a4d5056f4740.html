Hi PAt, good to hear from you.

Try remapping to the hard keys to BUTTON_R1 or BUTTON_L1

From Engineering:
BUTTON_R1 and BUTTON_L1 are reserved for scanning. You could remap any hardkeys to BUTTON_R1 and it will trigger the scanning.
