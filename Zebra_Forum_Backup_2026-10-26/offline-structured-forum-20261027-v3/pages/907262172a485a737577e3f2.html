Brian, The Vocollect client for the WT4090 needs both the keyboard and the display.&nbsp; The client maps the plus (+) and minus (-) keys that you find on a Vocollect T5 to the Up Arrow and Down Arrow keys on the WT4090.&nbsp; The VOW doesn't have these keys.&nbsp; Also, the status LED found on the T5 is a "virtual" LED light on the WT4090's display.&nbsp; Since the VOW doesn't have a display, you won't have a status LED.&nbsp; Attached is a picture that points out the keys used by the Vocollect client. I doubt if you'd have much luck getting Intermec/Vocollect to change the WT4090 client to support the VOW. Ken
