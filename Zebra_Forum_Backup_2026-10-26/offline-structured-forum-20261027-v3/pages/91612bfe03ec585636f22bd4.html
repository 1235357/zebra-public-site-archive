In that case, without doing a root cause analysis, I can provide a couple of hypothesis explaining this behavior. 1) The MC9090 and the MC3090 use different display hardware.&nbsp; The differences in the display drivers may be such that notifications are processed differently. 2) These are CE5 devices and Microsoft allows Motorola to customize the OS.&nbsp; Each of the product teams can independently select features to include in their product based upon the capabilities of each terminal.&nbsp; It may be that a certain feature selection in the MC3090 causes this behavior
