Hello,

I am integrating the RFD4031 2D barcode scanner with a custom Android app.

Using the most recent version (2.6.29.0) of the Scanner SDK for Android.

Everything seems to work fine and I am struggling with just one last issue: In some of the app's workflow I need to have a control of when a particular barcode was scanned. In particular, I need to make sure that a particular barcode was scanned while the RFD4031 scanner was actually connected to my app.

What I am seeing is that the RFD4031 can be used to scan barcodes by pulling the trigger button. Those barcodes seem to be stored in the scanners internal memory and then delivered to my app once the app connects to the scanner. In my app, I want to be able to ignore such cached barcodes. However, there doesn't seem to be any way to distinguish them from the barcodes scanned after the app connected to the scanner. They both get delivered via the same event and there's no timestamp or anything like that.

Is there any way how to either recognize the old barcodes when they are delivered to the Android app or to prevent the scanner from caching them while disconnected or to actively ask the scanner to clear its caches? (The RFID SDK for Android has com.zebra.api3.Actions.purgeTags() method that does this for the RFID tags.) 

Note: I have tried several workarounds like disabling the barcode scanning when disconnecting and reenabling upon reconnection as well as disabling/enabling particular barcode types so that they cannot even be scanned while disconnected. These solutions work to some degree but are not bulletproof. And they quite complicated compared to a single method call that exists for the RFID scanner.

Thank you for any ideas or guidance.

Honza
