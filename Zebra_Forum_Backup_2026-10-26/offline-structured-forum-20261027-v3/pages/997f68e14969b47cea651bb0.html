Yes, the MDM based updates to cradle and scanner firmware is a part of our design. We are working with Airwatch team on this support. And we are hopeful to include this in our FC release.

From the OS, we provide ways to invoke the cradle firmware update utility at command-line (without UI), so that its possible for updating cradle firmware without manual user intervention.
