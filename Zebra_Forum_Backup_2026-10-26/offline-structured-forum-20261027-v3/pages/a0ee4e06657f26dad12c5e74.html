It depends on the printer models. The user manual of the corresponding printer model should have the information about the supported label format languages. You can also verify this by using the Zebra Printer Setup Utility app, available on Google Play and Apple App Store. Or you can use the Printer Setup Utility for Windows by issuing the following SGD commands and looking for the options for the device.languages in the output.

### Get device info
! U1 getvar "device"
### Look for the options for the device.languages in the output to determine the supported label format languages.
.
.
.
device.jobs_print : 1 
device.languages : hybrid_xml_zpl , Choices: hybrid_xml_zpl,epl_zpl,epl,zpl
device.cph_enable : off , Choices: on,off
.
.
.
