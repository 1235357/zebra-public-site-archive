hi Carlos

I think we solved this offline last year- but in case others have similar questions I thought we could address this here.

Wr recently introduced some new features including the idea of Barcode Groups. Because of this change, Groups of Common Barcodes ( i.e., Barcodes that all begin with "1S")&nbsp; are only availble on Devices with SImulScan Engine 1.14x or greater.

Older versions of SimulScan ( Engine 1.13x or older)&nbsp; still support Any Barcode Solutions- but will not support Groups of Common Barcodes.

Older Devices can use Any Barcode-Fixed Quantity&nbsp; ( define fixed quantity 1-10)
The default templates for any 1, 2, 4, 5, or 10 are still available on your supported devices.
If anyone needs a Template for&nbsp; any 3, 6, 7, or 8 Barcodes- please send an email to NMV386@zebra.com

For anything above 10- this does require a device license and Device with SimulScan Engine 1.14x or Greater.
