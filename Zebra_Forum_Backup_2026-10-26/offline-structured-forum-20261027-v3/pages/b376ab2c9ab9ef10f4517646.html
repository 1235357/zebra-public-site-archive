Don, The only real change when moving to the MC65 from the MC75 should be to use COM6 instead of COM8. &nbsp;Both of the devices will be using the Microsoft gps driver to multiplex between applications and should behave the same. &nbsp; Prior to the WM6.5 BSP, COM8 on the MC75 went direct to the underlying gps driver (no multiplexing and only 1 app can access at a time). &nbsp;Besides the multiplexing, the application shouldn't be able to tell the difference between the WM61 and WM65 BSPs.
