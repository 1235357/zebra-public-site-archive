Make sure your ZD420 has the Bluetooth classic installed and enabled. You can use the following SGD command to check Bluetooth of your ZD420.

! U1 getvar "bluetooth"The output of the above SGD should look like something as shown below. Pay attention to the settings of bluetooth.discoverable,&nbsp;bluetooth.enable (they should be "on") and&nbsp;bluetooth.le.controller_mode (it should be "both" or "classic"). If you don't see anything for the setting of bluetooth.address, it means that this ZD420 does not have Bluetooth radio installed. Hope this helps.
