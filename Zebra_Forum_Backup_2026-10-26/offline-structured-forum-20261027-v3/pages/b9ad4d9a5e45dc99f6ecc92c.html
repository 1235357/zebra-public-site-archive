Sorry for the delayed response. The Xamarin team is NOT currently working on .NET MAUI support, but Zebra DOES respond to new feature requests. The process requires Zebra customers to contact their account representative and put in a formal request, which generally includes a description of the business case. The team evaluates the feature and prioritizes the work against their current workload to decide if and when to develop it.
