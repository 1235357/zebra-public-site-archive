Hi Cengiz,
Unfortunately Zebra does not yet have a Cordova or Phone-Gap plugin for our printers.&nbsp; It's my understanding that creating plugins using our Zebra Link-OS SDKs for these is not difficult.&nbsp; I believe that other software companies may have created these for their own use.&nbsp; I looked through the Cordova plugin listing, but I couldn't find anything posted.&nbsp; We have not received a large number of requests for the Cordova or Phone-Gap support yet.&nbsp; We have helped companies create their own plugins for some other cross-platform tools.&nbsp;&nbsp;
