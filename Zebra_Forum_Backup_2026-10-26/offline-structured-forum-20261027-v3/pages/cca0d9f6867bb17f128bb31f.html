Hi Austin,

Looking closely at the Postman snippet you provided, two quick adjustments should resolve the "Unknown Serial Number" error:

1. Update to the Production Endpoint
Your request is currently pointing to our staging environment (https://stage-api.zebra.com/...). Devices enrolled through the standard web portal are registered in our Production environment, which is why the staging API cannot find your printer's serial number.
Please update your POST request URL to:
https://api.zebra.com/v2/devices/printers/send

2. Check Header Syntax
In your snippet, there is an extra single quote at the end of the tenant header (--header 'tenant: MY-TENANT-ID'' \). Please double-check your headers in Postman to ensure there are no stray quotes or spaces, as this will cause the system to look for an incorrect tenant string.

Regarding the Swagger UI:
When using Swagger, please ensure that the "Servers" dropdown menu at the top of the page is set to Production (api.zebra.com) rather than Staging. Also, verify you have clicked the "Authorize" padlock and entered both your apikey and tenant headers correctly.

Give those adjustments a try, and let us know if you are still running into any issues!
