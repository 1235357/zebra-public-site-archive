Dino, &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Don't go down this path...it's painful!!!!&nbsp; Unfortunately, against my recommendation, a customer went down this path and is now in a less than desirable situation.&nbsp; Retention of the scanner unto the ET1 is the main issue resulting in the users abandoning the solution.&nbsp; The micro USB port was never intended for such a solution as there is no means of securing the cable and adapter. If you wish to test, I have been successful with an LS2208 (customer has stacked linear barcodes) using an adapter: 25-133854-01R (stay away from inexpensive adapters).&nbsp; I have not tested with the DS 4208, however, there is no reason why it would not work. The customer is now down the path of the scanner attachment.&nbsp; Though it is an imager and we are dealing with stacked linear codes, we were able to configure for "pick list" and all is well. Good luck, Don
