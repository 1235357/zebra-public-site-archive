My customer did some tests with some SIP clients including Cisco IP Blue. Th issue they faced with all clients was that the audio always goes through loudspeaker and there is no way to move it to the handset speaker. Per support - this is a known issue but officially we do not support any Voip clients in RevA image and this support will be added in future releases.
