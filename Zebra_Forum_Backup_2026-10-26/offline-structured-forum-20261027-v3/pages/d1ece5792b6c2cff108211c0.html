Any Link-OS printer model should have the full functionality supported by the Link-OS SDK for .NET. If you are looking for the low cost Link-OS printer models for the development, the ZQ310 or ZQ320 mobile printers are on the lower end of the cost. Please be aware that the ZQ310 and ZQ320 are the 2" and 3" in width respectively.
