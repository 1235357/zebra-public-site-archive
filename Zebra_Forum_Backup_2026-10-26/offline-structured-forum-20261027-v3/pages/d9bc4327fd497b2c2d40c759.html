The response from the getAvailableSettings() is printer model specific. Some models may have more options for some settings while some may have less options for some settings. Though the settings are printer model specific in general, a big portion of the settings are common across all printer models, such as settings related connectivity configurationl.

See the SGD (Set-Get-Do) section on the ZPL Programming Guide for more information on a specific setting. Some settings are read-write and some are read-only.
