Another Topic - Xamarin is no longer supported by Microsoft - and as a result Zebra No longer actively Maintains / Supports any Xamarin Implementation.  DOT - NET "MAUI" is the replacement / maintained DOT-NET / C# (C_Sharp) technology implementation.
