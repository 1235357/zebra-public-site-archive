Hi Justin, The quality of the media you're using may very well effect this. Does your RFID media have black marks on the back to indicate the gaps between labels?&nbsp; Mark media is less likely to have these issues.&nbsp; If you are not using Zebra media, we have run across other manufacturers who aren't the best at making sure the tag antenna is placed perfectly and consistently in the label.&nbsp; Also external lighting can sometimes have an effect on the process, especially the ZD printers. Try to make sure the lighting in the area with the printer is consistent and similar to how your customer will have it, especially if the media is not marked.&nbsp; In the end, you may need to switch media and/or adjust the label threshold&nbsp;setting.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;
Can you send the command below and post the image of what it prints? That might help determine if it's a gap(label length) or rfid calibration issue.

~JG&nbsp;
