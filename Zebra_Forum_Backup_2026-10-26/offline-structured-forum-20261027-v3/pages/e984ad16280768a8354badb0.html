Just wanted to share what I had to do. It seems when Mobile Device Center creats a connection, it creates a Network Adapter sometimes called Microsoft Virtual WiFi Adapter or Microsoft Windows Mobile Remote Adapter. When I was connecting the MCD device, nothing would happen. I would hear it accept, but not Sync. I went into the Properties of the network connection, uninstalled the driver. Then&nbsp;I re-connected my mobile device to my PC and it installed the drivers again and works again.
