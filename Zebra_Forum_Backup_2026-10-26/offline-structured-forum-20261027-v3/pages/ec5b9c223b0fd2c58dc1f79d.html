The attached files are a WT4090 skin for EveryWAN Remote Support Personal Edition( http://www.sparus-software.com/en/download/software/everywan-remote-sup…) which is a free , full featured remote control product from one of our ISV's. Feature set of this product is very similar to SOTI Pocket Controller but it is free of charge rather than being a licenced product. To use the skin, follow these steps: 1. Install EveryWAN Remote Support
2. Copy the skin files to C:\Program Files\Sparus Software\EveryWAN Remote Support Personal Edition\skin

 

3. You should get a prompt from the EveryWAN desktop application to reload the skin folder - answer Yes.

 

4. Check that the WT skin is listed under Symbol in the skin selector on the left side of the screen. Tick the checkbox beside the device name to enable the skin for the current device connection.
