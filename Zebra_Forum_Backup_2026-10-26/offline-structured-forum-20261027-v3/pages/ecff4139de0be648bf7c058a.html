All, The Fusion 2.55.1-16R release to support the new MAC address pool says it works with BSP26 or BSP30.  Since the WT4090 never had a BSP26 release, just a BSP20 release, I checked with the Support Center to see if this Fusion build would work with BSP20.  I was told no (see response below).  This means that the 2.55.1-16R will only work with BSP30 on the WT4090. Support Center response:  Per MCD-ECRT, Fusion v2.55.1-16R is not supported on WT4090’s operating on BSP20.  BSP20 is no longer supported, & the respective customer must update their WT4090 devices to BSP30 in order to allow for supported installation of Fusion v2.55.1-16R.  There are no exceptions…
