Hi Sato, at Coop I run fw v. 1.3.1 on RFS7000 and v2.2.1 on AP5131. On ACS, if I want to put a specific user on VLAN 8&nbsp; -say- , I will set: Attribute 64 (Tunnel-Type) to VLAN Attribute 65 (Tunnel-Medium-Type) to 802 Attribute 81 ( Tunnel Private Group ID) to 8 This is regardless of the name I give to VLAN 8 on AP5131. Good luck Ugo
