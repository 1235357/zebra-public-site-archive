Hi Christophe,

I suggest you check out the KeyMap manager documentation: Keymapping Manager - Zebra Technologies TechDocs , you should be able to remap any of the hardware keys as you require, specifically you will want the "Send Trigger" "Key Behaviour Identifier" applied to the volume button I guess.

Do you really want to MOVE the button?&nbsp; Maybe configure your device to be ambidextrous so you do not have to worry about specific people having to use specific devices.&nbsp; You should be able to also use the KeyMap manager to change the behaviour of the PTT button, should you wish to.
