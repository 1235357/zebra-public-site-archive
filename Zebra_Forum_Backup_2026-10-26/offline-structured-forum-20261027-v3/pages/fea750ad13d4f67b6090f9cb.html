Don these are modified debounce settings for CE7 device.&nbsp; Maybe you can look to see what the default values you have and tweak from there, or google the keys and see what other info might be posted. [HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Drivers\BuiltIn\KEYBD] "DebounceCounter"=dword:18 [HKEY_CURRENT_USER\ControlPanel\Keybd] "InitialDelay"=dword:28A
