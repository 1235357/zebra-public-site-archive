Hi Dennis,

What printer are you using for your implementation? The ZPL RFID commands are dependent on Zebra printer model, please, check the link below to verify compatibility.

https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/zebra/manuals/printers/common/program…

Here, below a link where you can select the right RFID manual for the right printer.

RFID Programming Guide for Zebra Printers | Zebra

For example, if you are using a ZD500R model, please, check the link below for the right RFID manual, and take a look at pages 60, 61, 62 &amp; 63 for knowing how to use ^RL command for kill password.

https://support.zebra.com/cpws/docs/ait/KA_files/8973/rfid3-pg-en.pdf
