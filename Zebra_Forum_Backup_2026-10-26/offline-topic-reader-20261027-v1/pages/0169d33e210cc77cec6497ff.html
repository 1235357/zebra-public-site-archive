                  Hello!I'm working on an android application for the RFC40 scanning device, and been following the documentation for enabling RFID and Barcode scanning. I've had success enabling RFID scanning but when I attempt to switch to barcode scanning, I don't seem to be any events triggered when I scan a barcode. I've essentially followed the documentation here -> https://techdocs.zebra.com/dcs/rfid/android/2-0-5-226/samples/fx_hellorfid/ the only difference I have is I have invoked reader.Config.setTriggerMode(BARCODE_MODE, true); after device connection.The barcode scanner appears to work and beeps when I scan a barcode, but there are no events triggered in the RfidEventsListener and therefore I don't receive the scanned barcode in my project. Where could I be going wrong?

The HHSampleApp does work to (for basics), but 123RFID Mobile (Android - or iOS) - shows all the features.
