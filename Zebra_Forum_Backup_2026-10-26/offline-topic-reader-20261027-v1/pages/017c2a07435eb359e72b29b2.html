            Afshin,   &nbsp;Check the names of the SCT and RMT files which they are using - the names were changed for some reason between WM6,1 and WM6.5 e.g&nbsp;   WM6.1:&nbsp;&nbsp; QWS.SCT , QWR.RMT   WM6.5&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; QWERTYS.SCT , QWERTYR.RMT   &nbsp;If you rename the oriiginal files then they may work on the MC9190 although it is probably a good idea&nbsp;to rebuild the files using the 9190 KBtools (which should&nbsp;be available via Tech Support).   Best Regards,  Ian Hatton
