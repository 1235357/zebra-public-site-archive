            Hi, here's the answer to your question on behalf of the team:
The SDK only support Android version 9, 10 and 11 officially. It is possible the SDK will work on older Android OS version, but they will not be supported if problem exist, unless the problem also exist on Android version 9, 10 or 11.
Yes snapi is the com protocol.
No older versions of the SDK are posted. Only the latest is available.
