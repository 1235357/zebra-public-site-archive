            Hi there,
Unfortunately we do not share anything public on how to unlock the bootloader of our devices and as you mentioned it's strictly for security purposes.  We do however provide ways to temporarily do it in case the system or any partition for some reason is getting corrupted blocking the normal booting sequence of a device - And in that case you'll have to flash separately each partition with fastboot.
Dan
