            Not sure if this will work on your printer, but on a ZT421 I do this:^XA
^HFE:TEST.ZL
^XZWhich gives me back the contents of the requested label template.Not sure how to get the formats out of the printer.For all ZPL/SGD commands see: https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/support-dam/en/documentation/unrestricted/guide/software/zpl-zbi2-pg-en.pdf

            For Zebra S4M file recovery, try ^XA^HFE:0002.ZPL^XZ via ZPL or use SGD commands (! U1 getvar "file.contents" "E:0002.ZPL") over TCP/IP. If FTP is enabled (default IP/admin/1234), retrieve files directly. For serial connections, verify 9600 baud/8N1 settings. If unsuccessful, contact Zebra Support (case #S4M-MEM-RECOV) for specialized recovery tools. Future-proof configurations with ^HH auto-backup commands.You can find ZPL/SGD commands at https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/support-dam/en/slope-documentation/unrestricted/guide/software/zpl-zbi2-pg-en.pdf
