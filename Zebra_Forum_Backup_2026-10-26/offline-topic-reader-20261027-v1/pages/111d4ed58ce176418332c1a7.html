            There has been a similar issue addressed in SPR 16628. SPB manual/help file is made available along with installable copy of Pocket Browser (up on support central). I have attached a PB v2.2 help file in here for your quick reference.&nbsp; Check either IE-&gt;Options-&gt;Security (For WM) or Internet Options-&gt;Advanced (For CE) settings/configuration.
Also take a look at the 'Error Dlg Displayed On Every Error' (Windows CE Only) or 'ShowScriptErrors' registry keys (might be helpful, documented in help file).
