                  One of my customers is seeing disruptions while accessing the GPS data on a MC55.  They have an application that keeps the GPS comport open all of the time and collects coordinates every minute.  The problem is that some devices stop reporting coordinates during the day.  If the application is stopped and started the data points are accessible.    These devices also have SOTI loaded on them and I’m leaning toward this as being the issue.  
 Has anyone else had an issue with SOTI being loaded on a device and had interruptions of collecting GPS data?
