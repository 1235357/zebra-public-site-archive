                  A client has reported their MC75As (WM6.5.3) and MC95s (WM6.1) running extremely slow after several hours in operations. Running eMscript logs clearly show connmgr.exe consuming 85% of CPU. Moto engineering is aware of the problem but needs a sample application that reproduces the problem in order to chase after Microsoft (although there are dozens of cases reported on the internet on this very issue).     Anyhow, as it relates to this client, the challenge is that WWAN connection is required along with a server which is only reachable via private APN in Canada. Even if we were able to build the app environment in NY, it would not connect hence unable to replicate the negative effects of connmgr.exe.     The purpose for this post is to see if anyone else encountered this issue and if workarounds or best practices were identified.    Any feedback is most appreciated.

            When I saw this issue the CPU would peg and lock the device up.&nbsp; When I loaded the newer OS with a different AKU the issue went away. Loads with an AKU of 5.3.9 or higher the issue went away.&nbsp; I have not heard of the issue with WM6.5.3
