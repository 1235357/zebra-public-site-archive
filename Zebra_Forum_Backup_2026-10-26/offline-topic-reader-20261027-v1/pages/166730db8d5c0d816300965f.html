            Our Android devices support USB OTG which allows them to act as a USB host with a suitable cable e.g. for the TC55 use an OTG cable such as the one shown here: https://www.amazon.co.uk/female-Micro-Adapter-Cable-Function/dp/B009DFC……
With this adaptor cable , I think you can use an external USB fingerprint reader such as the one shown here :
https://idency.com/products/futronic-fingerprint-readers/futronic-fs80-……
This reader has an Android driver/app which should be compatible with the TC55 but this would need to be tested in order to ensure compatibility.
