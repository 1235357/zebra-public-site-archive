                  Hi All,   I'm working with an airline customer that is interested in tracking the status and location of all their vehicles at the airport (i.e. tractors, de-icers, tugs, etc.).  They need to know where the vehicle is located (i.e. GPS) and its status (i.e. is vehicle ignition on or off to indicate that the vehicle is in use).  The information can be provided from the vehicle via either WiFi or cellular.  I had originally thought the VC6096 may work, but is overkill based on what the customer wants to do.  They are looking for more of a black box solution that just relays the vehicle location and status info back to a server.  The 'black box' will be powered from the vehicle.    Has anyone run into a similar request or may be aware of a solution that may work?   Thanks,  Ken
