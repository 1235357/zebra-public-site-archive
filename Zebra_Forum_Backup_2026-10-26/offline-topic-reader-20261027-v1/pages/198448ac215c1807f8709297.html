            1. These controls are currently not avaialble for VS2008.&nbsp; They are expected in a future release. 2. The help file in EMDK for .NET&nbsp;provides information&nbsp;on these controls under the section "Symbol Design Time Components".&nbsp; EMDK also installs 3 CS + 3 VB samples illustrating the use of these controls: CS_BarcodeControlSample1, CS_ImagingControlSample1 and CS_MagstripeContolSample1. The corresponding VB sample begin with "VB_" instead of "CS_".
