            Justin,   &nbsp;If you enable GPS in the ArcSoft camera app, it will store the GPS co-ordinates in the EXIF header of the captured image. This header is included in the JPG file generated by the camera and can be extracted and viewed on a PC using a suitable utility e.g. Jhead ( http://www.sentex.net/~mwandel/jhead/).
