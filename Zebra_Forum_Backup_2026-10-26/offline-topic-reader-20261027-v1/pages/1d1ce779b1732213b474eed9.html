            Use the RemCapture tool to look at how setting changes affect the registry.&nbsp; Start by connecting the MC75 and taking a copy of the registry settings.&nbsp; Change the Bluettoth settings on the MC75 and then do a compare of the registry.&nbsp; This will point you in the right direction.&nbsp; Note that some devices have two different Bluetooth drivers so you may need to play around to make sure you have the right driver on / off.&nbsp; I don't know the settings for the MC75 however here are the setting we have used for the VC70 to turn Bluetooth off, so probably similar to what you are after.
;***************************************
; Turn off Blue Tooth&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; *
;***************************************
[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\SymbolBluetooth\MotoBTUI\Config]
"CEMSStackBthRadioMode" = dword:00000000
[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\SymbolBluetooth]
"MSStackLastState" = dword:00000000
[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\SOFTWARE\Microsoft\Bluetooth\sys]
"ScanMode" = dword:00000000
"Power" = dword:00000000
