                  I have a customer who is trying to write a driver for a WLAN card for the MC1000. They have done this in the past and are making a new one for a newer model.
They have two questions (below) which I don't know how to answer. There are more on the way from their engineering group and I will update this thread when I get them.
1. What is the reference platform on which MC1000 is based upon? 
2. The SD bus driver shipped with MC1000 is the MS SD 1.0 bus driver, the latest SD bus driver from MS is 2.0. Is there a limitation on the platform to support SDHC which is part of MS SD 2.0 driver specification?
