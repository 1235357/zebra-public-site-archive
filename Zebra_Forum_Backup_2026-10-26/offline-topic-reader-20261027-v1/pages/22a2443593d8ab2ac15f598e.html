            We cannot get the MIB directly from a non QLnXXX printer. Only the QLnXXX printer models provides the ability for getting the MIB directly from the printers. For all other printer models, the MIB file is downloadable from the support page.
For Link-OS printer models, the MIB can be downloaded from the firmware section of the respective support page of that printer model.
For non Link-OS printer models, such as the GX or GK series, the MIB can be downloaded from the Support for the ZebraNet 10/100 Print Server page.
