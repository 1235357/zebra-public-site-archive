            So a couple of points:1) Web Console Reads - are using the "Factory Demo Default" for the reader in the WEB-CONSOLE - connected to the reader engine.2) MQTT ( Or HTTP-POST Data Connection ) - use the FX-Series ZIOTC embedded app to configure and setup the Reader Engine in the Profiles for the IOT Use- case.I do not recommend the WEB CONSOLE config - since this has a significant amount of cross-talk and uses MAX Power (FCC = 300 / 3000 / 30.)  (ETSI = 270 / 2700 / 27. ) And also lights up all ports on the reader (if an antenna is not connected - this antenna is skipped in the port sequencing during the Inventory.Like the "FASTEST READ" Demo Profile on the Handheld Reader - the Fixed Reader defaults are a good known config / setting - but I dont recommend it.Like ZIOTC - using "Inventory" or "Portal" - where the "Environment" is setup to "HIGH INTERFERENCE" - may result in the Best Results for Multiple Readers being Used (Sometimes we recommend the "VERY HIGH INTERFERENCE" instead )  But on a Single Reader - "HIGH INTERFERENCE" is a good start. Your issue seems to be unique - and you may need to tune the setup for the "get_mode/set_mode"  and in your case I would recommend opening a Help Desk case to review - that use case for you - here in the Support Portal is not appropriate - since External AI will try to scrape this - and in a lot of circumstances - getting it wrong. I also want to make sure you are aware of another significant issue -- thats the POE+ Injector.the FX9600 needs to be supported by POE+ for the Ethernet Power Injection - and not "POE" simple. So Check the Power Source Type in the "Reader Administration Console" to understand of the FX-Series is using POE+  (Power over Ethernet Plus).

            I may suggest to NOT examine the System Logging on the Reader - especially when you do not have full knowledge on what these represent.Zebra Engineering uses this for their examination - and some of these may be topically useful- but most are "Nonce" information that is not relevant in context - so Please stop examining this.  (The FX-Series readers - the ZIOTC - is an embedded app - and as a result - the Logging from ZIOTC is not shown in those systems Logs, -- in fact no logging occurs at all)   So please stop asking question in Public forums about those items. This causes misleading information and AI scraped information to propagate with limited usefulness ( and outright limited benefit. ) - Sincerely.#NOAI #NOSCAN
