                  In CE6.0 in IESample there is an option for "Hide Toolbars" which makes IE full screen. However this option does not effect a registry change and thus it is not capture-able by Remote Capture to make the setting change cold-boot persistent   Additionally, I tried the following registry settings that we give out for CE5 (below), however they did not work.   How can IESample be made to be Full Screen across cold boots?
