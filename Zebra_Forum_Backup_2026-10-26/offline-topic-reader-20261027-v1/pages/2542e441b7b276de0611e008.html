Issue - Camera is always on.

            Hi, all Android Zebra mobile computers (including the TC57) come with DataWedge, it should be available from the app launcher.&nbsp; To disable DataWedge, launch the app, then press the 3 dots then settings.&nbsp; From the settings menu you can disable DataWedge to prevent the scanner enabling until you launch it with the EMDK.
