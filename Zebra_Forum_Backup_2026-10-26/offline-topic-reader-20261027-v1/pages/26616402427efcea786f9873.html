            If your device goes into critical suspend, the device will resume with the radio disabled.&nbsp; It also can happen if the wifi sleep policy is set to never disable.&nbsp; This happened on both the ET1 and MC40.   For the MC40, it was fixed with this CQ  [CQCM]  SR EMBPD00084974 [F][-] - [BETA] MC40 WLAN disabled after Critical Suspend,&nbsp;&nbsp; Contact Venkey to see if this patch is released into a BSP yet.   For the ET1, it was fixed via SPR 23315.
