            The IOT Post/Batch Posting does not provide what you seek as a "Startup" condition - the IOT will always send the initial data FIRST - when started up and then postings will follow the timing you seek.Also please make sure you are operating with Up-To-Date Firmware - improvements to the IoT Framework on the FX-Series readers is steadily ongoing.
