            Hi Aki

step #1, check the current BSP  (Android image) installed on your device (under Settings / About phone / Build number) vs. the latest available BSP listed here https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/software/operating-system/tc27-operating-system-for-gms-devices.html; if they differ, download and upgrade to the latest version. Possibly factory reset the device. 
step #2, if #1 does not solve your issue, create a small Android project that replicates the issue and share it on Github.
