                  We are using the MAUI Zebra Printer SDK to print labels to LINK-OS printers and have had reports from users that if you try to print an image, even a small one around ~20kb, the command to PrintImage takes around 45 seconds each time to print the label. On Android and Windows, it prints immediately.We verified the behavior in our app and also using the Zebra MauiDevDemo. In both, it prints immediately on Windows and Android takes 45+ seconds on iOS.Sample CodeZebraImageI image = Zebra.Sdk.Graphics.ZebraImageFactory.GetImage(signaturePath);printer.PrintImage(image, 0, 0, image.Width, image.Height, false);Test SetupSDK: Zebra.Printer.SDK 3.0.3271 and 3.0.3295Dev Platform: .NET MAUI 8Printer: ZD420Devices: iPad 10 iOS 18.2, iPhone 15 iOS 18.2
