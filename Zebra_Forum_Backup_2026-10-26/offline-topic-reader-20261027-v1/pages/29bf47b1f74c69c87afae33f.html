We checked RtLog for "ErrHandler: ARM9&nbsp; FATAL ERROR" and found that the random reboot occurred 13 times during 12:32:47 ~12:47.&nbsp; &nbsp;It looked that the warm boot could not recover the device so cold boot was necessary. We found the random reboot was linked with the failure of entering suspend(12:31:48) which caused auto reboot begun at 12:31:48. &nbsp;We also found that system resumed at 12:31:47 but the resuming log message looked abnormal and moreover system immediately entered suspend again at 12:31:48.  Engineering requested to have QXDM done.
