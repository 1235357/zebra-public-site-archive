            There are three default Profiles loaded on Zebra Android devices with DataWedge:Profile0,  Launcher, DWDemo .Also this TechDoc may be of Help to You:https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/13-0/guide/about/There are two demo apps for Android Studio I recommend for first-time developers using Zebra Android / DataWedge.See the Basic Intent demo.  And see the Data Capture demo -- from this samples page:https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/13-0/guide/samples/ Both of these are Android Studio samples - but you can try online / external links to look at Xamarin Forms of these as well as versions that may support MAUI implementations.
