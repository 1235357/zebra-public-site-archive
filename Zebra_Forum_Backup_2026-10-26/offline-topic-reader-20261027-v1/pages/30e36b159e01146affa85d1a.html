                  I am implementing an Android application that connects a phone with Zebra DS2278 Scanner using SSI Bluetooth Low Energy. I can generate a barcode using dcssdkGetPairingBarcode function. Scanning this barcode pairs and connects the scanner. Great. But when I disconnect the scanner using dcssdkTerminateCommunicationSession, I cannot undo this with dcssdkEstablishCommunicationSession (it returns error 2: DCSSDK_RESULT_SCANNER_NOT_AVAILABLE). That's strange. I would expect this to swiftly reconnect the scanner (which is already paired). My second problem is that when I close the app and reopen, the SDK fails to find the scanner and I cannot reconnect the device without generating the barcode and pairing again. Needless to say, pairing anew on every app launch with barcode is too clumsy to be used in production.I already spent many hours trying to find a solution with no avail, so I appreciate any help. DS 2278 ScannerGoogle Pixel 9aAndroid 16Zebra Scanner SDK for Android 2.7.3.0Building with macOS 26.5 in Android Studio Quail 1
