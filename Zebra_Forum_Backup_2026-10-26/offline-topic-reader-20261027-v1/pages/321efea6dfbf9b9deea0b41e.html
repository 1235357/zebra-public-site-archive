            Hi William, 
If you cannot get the Admin privilege from EHS and StageNow is not among the Kiosk APPs, it's nearly impossible to get pass the Kiosk Mode.
Only viable solution is either to uninstall EHS remotely if the device is under an EMM or to reset the device.  
Another solution is to extract the EHS configuration from the device, either from the EMM or through ADB (But I assume this is disabled) and check the code/password for the Admin mode, which has been used in the xml.
 
Dan
