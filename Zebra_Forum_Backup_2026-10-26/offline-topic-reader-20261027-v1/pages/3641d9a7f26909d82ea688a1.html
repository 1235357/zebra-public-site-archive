            Hi,
Our documentation can be found on TechDocs: https://techdocs.zebra.com/
For configuration, we have a service called MX: https://techdocs.zebra.com/mx/ & a utility application called StageNow: https://techdocs.zebra.com/stagenow/5-5/about/
In terms of using the scanner, we recommend using DataWedge: https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/11-2/guide/about/
If you have any specific questions, please feel free to post another thread on here & someone will respond.
