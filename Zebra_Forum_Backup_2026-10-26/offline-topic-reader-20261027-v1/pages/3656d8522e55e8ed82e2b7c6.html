            I seem to have gotten past this issue.  I'm not certain as to whether the problem was caused by a too-long path, or if there was some issue with the DLLs being included where they should not have been.The changes I made in my project were:Moved the "References" folder containing the zebra DLLs for each platform to be at the root of my solutionAdded the following to my project file:    <ItemGroup>
      <Compile Remove="References\**" />
      <EmbeddedResource Remove="References\**" />
      <MauiCss Remove="References\**" />
      <MauiXaml Remove="References\**" />
      <None Remove="References\**" />
    </ItemGroup>In addition, I deleted the 'obj' and 'bin' folders to get a completely clean slate.  Anyway, given that Dotnet 9 is now out of support, it seems odd that Zebra has not released a version of this package that specifically targets Dotnet 10.
