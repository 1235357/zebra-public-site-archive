            Jon,  &nbsp;You&nbsp;may be able to put this together using the Cradlepoint USB to Ethernet adaptor detailed at http://www.cradlepoint.com/category-lists/janam. This would connect to the MC75 USB cable via a USB A female/female adaptor and may work without an external PSU. Drivers should load automatically provided the MC75 is switched to USB host mode .             Hi all...  I have a potential case where the 75 could be put in the field as an FTP server to upgrade firmware: this case assumes a direct cable connection between the 75 and the device being flashed.  Other than the inVision cradle, which is a bit clunkly for this application, has anyone come across an inline ethernet cable for the 75? If we had a way to plug the device straight into the Ethernet port of the target device, that would be more perfect than words!&nbsp;   Cheers, -JP
