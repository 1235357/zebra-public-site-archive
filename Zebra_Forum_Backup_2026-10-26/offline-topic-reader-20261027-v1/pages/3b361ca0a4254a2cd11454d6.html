                  Hello...   I got a call from a customer who currently has MC55XX's in a hospital and getting ready to purchase another 175 MC55AO's.&nbsp;   They are having an issue where the MC55's (old and new) are intermittently charing in their 4 slot cradles.&nbsp; In a single slot cradle all the devices charge fine.&nbsp; When placing the device in the 4 slot cradle it may charge in a slot, but when moved to a differnt slot it will not charge, but when putting a different MC55 in the slot that wouldn't just charge the previous unit, it charges the next.&nbsp; The customer stated that sometimes if you "push down" on the unit, it will charge while it's being held down.   Tried multiple 4 slot cradles with multiple old and new MC55's&nbsp;with the same&nbsp;intermittent&nbsp;results, can't find a single failed component.&nbsp; Going on site on Monday to investigate.   Any thoughts or suggestions?   Thanks...   Jesse
