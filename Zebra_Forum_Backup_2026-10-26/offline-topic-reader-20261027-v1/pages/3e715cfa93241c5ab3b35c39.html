            Mike,  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Tough one to provide a clear answer.&nbsp; It appears to be somewhat binary in behavior, On/Off.&nbsp; From a developer's perspective it pays to delve into understanding the behavior as best as possible and applying the controls as desired.    To avoid the development nightmare look into one of the popular VPN clients for WM as some provide a level of control over arbitration of the connection.&nbsp; The user can determine under what conditions which network is selected.&nbsp; Sort of a poor man's "least cost route"&nbsp;measure!!             MS will maintain a connection as long as it can. Thus if the cellular connection was made last at the device still sees this connection it will use it. For the device to release the cellular connection it will need to lose this connection.&nbsp; How do you do this?&nbsp; Disable the card or go out of range.&nbsp; Once a connection is lost MS will test hardwired, wifi, cellular for a connection. &nbsp;Once it finds one it will stick with it until it is gone.
Hope this helps.
