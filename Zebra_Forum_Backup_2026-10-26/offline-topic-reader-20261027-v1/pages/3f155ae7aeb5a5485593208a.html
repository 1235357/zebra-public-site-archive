                  We've recently purchased a Zebra ZC 300 printer at my job and we need it to print a ZPL template.
We're using D365 Finances and they recently released a new feature for printing labels using ZPL templates, but one of our label needs to be printed on a card.
We thought all Zebra printers supported ZPL format, but after a quick chat with support, I was told that card printers do not...
And then I was advised to post here for help on finding a solution...

I have tried adding the printer with the Generic text-only driver, and nothing happens when I try to print using it.
With the correct driver, it prints normal non-ZPL just fine... but I really need it to print ZPL because that's what D365 offers me.

            No, ZPL is not accepted by the ZC300 series card printers. Label printer are completely different from the card printers. ZPL doesn't work with the card printers. You may try to find a way to send the resulting image of the ZPL label to the card printer, since the card printers support image printing.
