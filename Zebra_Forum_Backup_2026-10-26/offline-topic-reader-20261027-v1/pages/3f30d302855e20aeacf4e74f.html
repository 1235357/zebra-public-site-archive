            Check you symbology configuration to make sure that I 2 of 5 is disabled (or set to the specific length or range).&nbsp; If I2of5 is enabled for all lengths, you can get what appear to be mis-decodes but is really just the scanner seeing what looks like an I2of5 barcode data.
