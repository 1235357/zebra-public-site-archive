            solved this already, it seems TA who sent me this unit had it flashed with wrong Images.   so FYI&nbsp;   if&nbsp; DVT unit is flashed with&nbsp; MVT 19.0&nbsp; OS version the following behavior &nbsp;can be observed.   when device has totally empty battery it will say first " battery too low , shutdown device " if you leave it on AC charger it will end with&nbsp; "Charger Disabled"   resolution is to&nbsp; reflash&nbsp; with DVT 19.0 Only images and then the device will be able to charge a totally empty&nbsp; battery.   adviced TA to not reflash the device anymore as well :-)
