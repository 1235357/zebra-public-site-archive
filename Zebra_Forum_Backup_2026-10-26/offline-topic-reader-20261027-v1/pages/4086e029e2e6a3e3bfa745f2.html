            Is this for a Printer application (RFID),  Fixed Application?, Or Handheld Application?A basic question such as this; the only logical choice is to enumerate the available tag(s) in field - and then based on the enumerated  EPC for the tags - write/update each tag as you filter for them / choose the specific EPC from the previous inventory capture.So if you only have one EPC inventoried - you choose that EPC - and update the "EPC" memory bank (With correct EPC offsetting) and update the tag.
