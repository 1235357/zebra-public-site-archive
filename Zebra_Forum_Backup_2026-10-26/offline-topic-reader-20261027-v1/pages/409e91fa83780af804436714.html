            What you are seeing is normal behavior. The Manufacturing template is an example of a "Barcodes only" template. Since no text or image data is required, only barcode scanning, the imager is used rather than the camera. If you print the Manufacturing label (available from the How to Use This Demo screen) and scan it, you will see that all five barcodes are decoded simultaneously and reported with a single good decode beep.
