            I've been able to find the solution to the exception on connection.Open(). It was caused by not getting permissions for the USB device (printer.Manager.HasPermission &amp; printer.Manager.RequestPermission). After getting the user's permission in Android to use the USB printer the connection.Open() works as expected.
