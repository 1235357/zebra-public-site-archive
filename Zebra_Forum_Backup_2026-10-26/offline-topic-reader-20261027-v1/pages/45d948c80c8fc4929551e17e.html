            Hi Grant,
Yes, this is possible and you are looking at the right printer, but there are two important clarifications for your setup:
Hardware (BLE vs. Classic): The ZD510-HC is a great choice, but its standard BLE is for configuration only and cannot accept print jobs. To print on demand without internet, you must order the ZD510-HC model that includes the Classic Bluetooth option.
Software (Link-OS): Yes, the Link-OS Multiplatform SDK is the correct path. Because Zebra provides native SDKs for iOS and Android, you will just need to use a wrapper (like Cordova or Capacitor) to bridge our native SDKs to your Ionic application.
You can find the SDK downloads and documentation here: Link-OS Multiplatform SDK
