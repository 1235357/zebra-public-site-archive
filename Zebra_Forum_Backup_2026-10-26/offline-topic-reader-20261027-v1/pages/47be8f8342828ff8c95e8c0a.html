            Hello - I may suggest asking this in a Google Search as follows:"GPIO SENSOR FX9600" "GPIO LIGHT FX9600" for compatible devices - and rather than use the FX9600 adaptor - follow the guidelines carefully for the FX7500 GPIO pinout and use the FX7500 GPIO connector instead.PLEASE NOTE: The GPIO is highly recommended to use the DC external Power Supply - which provides the 24V supply voltage to the GPIO - otherwise you will encounter problems if you assume the GPIO will work with only POE/POE+ - which the GPIO Pinout cannot supply the required Voltage (since this is dependent on the 24V DC PSU Supply).
