            Customer's Question:&nbsp; the only subsystem that will actively turn itself off is the WAN radio.&nbsp; The specific threshold is approximately 11%.&nbsp; Please note that this threshold could be reached while the terminal is suspended.&nbsp; The WAN module itself monitors the voltage and will turn itself off independently of the host system.&nbsp; Keep in mind that the instantaneous voltage on the battery is dependent upon the current draw.&nbsp; So, even if the voltage drops below 11% and the modem turns off, the voltage could raise above this threshold because the modem has turned off.  Subquestion 1: No.  Sub-question 2: Once the WAN radio has turned off, the user must manually turn it back on.
