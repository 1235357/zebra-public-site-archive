            The reason DWDemo comes up if you scan from the launcher is that the Launcher profile in DataWedge is set up to send scan data to the intent that DwDemo is waiting for.&nbsp; If your app uses datawedge and is receiving scan data in an intent, you could modify the launcher profile to send the data to your app instead.
https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/7-6/guide/about/
https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/7-6/guide/output/intent/
