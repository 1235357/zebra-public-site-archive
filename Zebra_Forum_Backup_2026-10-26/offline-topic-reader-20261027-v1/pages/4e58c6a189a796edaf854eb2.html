            I had the same problem with another customer.  The Camera and the Scanner on our devices can't be active at the same time.&nbsp; They share a input port on the processor and must be multiplexed.&nbsp; This was the response that I received from ECRT when I asked the question.  If you want to use the Camera and the DataWedge in the same application, then you can use the DataWedge's SDK to send Enable/Disable messages to the DataWedge.  Thus, you'd send an "Disable" to the Wedge, take your picture, and then send an "Enable" to the Wedge from the application.
