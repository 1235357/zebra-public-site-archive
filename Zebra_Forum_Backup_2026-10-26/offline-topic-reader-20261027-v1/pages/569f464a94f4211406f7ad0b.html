            Hi Afshin, check the following page on the WNS Sales Rack. Scroll down to the bottom for a few mot-on-mot links.   I have seen first hand the differences between our MC9090 and VC5090 devices vs. Vocollect T5 Talkman devices with less then stellar radios and yes our devices clearly performed better, mostly in roaming when using WPA2 with AES and PEAP w/ MSCHAPv2. Vocollect had roaming issues causing "beeping" on their devices while Mot devices were fine. This was on an RFS7000 in a DC back on v1.3.2   http://compass.mot-solutions.com/web/wnssales/Competition
