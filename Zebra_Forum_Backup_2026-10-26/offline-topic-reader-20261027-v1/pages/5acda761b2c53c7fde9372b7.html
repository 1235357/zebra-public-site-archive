            DataWedge is part of the Operating System image and can be updated through an OS update.&nbsp; The latest LifeGuard patch for the TC70 is 02.10.02-LG12 and includes DataWedge 6.7:&nbsp;https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/zebra_new_ia/en-us/software/operating…
LifeGuard updates can be obtained from the Support Portal&nbsp;https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/mobile-computers/handheld… aware you will need a support contract to download these images.
