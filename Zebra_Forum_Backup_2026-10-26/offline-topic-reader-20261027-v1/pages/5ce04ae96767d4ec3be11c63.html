                  Customer has deployed a large number of MC75 (GPRS, BSP27). They had a custom APN on the unit but recently removed it and are going with standard APN (wap.cingular).   On some of their devices, when they try to connect for data, they are prompted with the Connection Manager Login Screen (User Name, PW and Domain). In their configuration these are all blank and they are not prompted for Login.  The only way they can connect is to remove the SIMM. Once the SIMM is out, they can put any data into the login screen and replace the SIMM. At that point the can connect.   Not sure if this is an AT&T issue having to do with authentication in fringe locations.   We are going to run RilDebug Logger to see if we can see anything that does not seem correct.   Any ideas?
