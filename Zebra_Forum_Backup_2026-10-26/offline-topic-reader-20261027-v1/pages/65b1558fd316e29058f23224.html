            Hi Daniele,
Unfortunately the answer is the same across most bar codes.&nbsp; Just using ZPL in the printer there is no way to set a fixed width. You can use a label creation tool, especially with a fixed data barcode like EAN-13, to manage the size, but not in ZPL directly.&nbsp;
