            Your `java.lang.UnsatisfiedLinkError` when connecting USB devices via the Zebra SDK after packaging indicates that the JVM cannot find or load required native DLLs from inside your JAR. The solution is to **deploy these DLL files externally, alongside your executable JAR**, rather than embedding them. The JVM requires DLLs to be accessible on the file system at runtime, not within an archive.
