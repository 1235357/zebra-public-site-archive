                  Just wanted to check why there is a difference between the default button mapping for accessing the list of recent apps when comparing the TC70 with the TC55 KitKat? On the TC55 KK OS , the recent apps list is accessed via a long press of the menu button (third from left on the soft panel). On the TC70 , the same recent apps list is accessed via a long press of the home button and long pressing the menu button has no effect. Would be better to have a consistent mapping between the products especially for customers who may implement both TC55 and TC70/75's.
