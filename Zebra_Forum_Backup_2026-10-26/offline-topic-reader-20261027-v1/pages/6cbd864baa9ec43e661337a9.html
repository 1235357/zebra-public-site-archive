                  10:55/05-15-2009 3day MC7506 WM6.1 1886008  I received this request:   One of our Business Partners would like to know is that a way to totally disable the WWAN (phone) on MC7506? They can not accept registry value as this is not safe where user can remove the registry from the unit via ActiveSync. What user want is basically make the unit to batch mode. Is that a way we can remove the RIL file or disable the phone?  As far as I know the RIL cannot be removed. But if SIM is not installed, and the Phone is turned off via Wireless Manager, it will not turn on on warm or cold boot. So my recommendation would be to use Appcenter to prevent end users from accessing options to turn Phone on. I am looking for any other options I may be overlooking> o
