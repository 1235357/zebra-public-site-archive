            Hi Damian, unfortunately that component was developed a long time ago outside of Zebra coverage. 
One thing you could do is to 

export your profile from an older device 
on the new device, restore datawegde (it factory-resets DW)
import the exported profile into the newer device

Double-check that all the plugins are well-defined. Specifically, check the barcode input plugin and the Intent Output one: the intent action, category, and send method must match those in your code.
Finally, to understand if your app running on newer devices is receiving intents as expected, send that intent manually from adb following the usual schemaadb shell am broadcast -a <action> -d <data_uri> -t <mime_type> -c <category> -e <extra_key> <extra_value> -n <component>
The two actions above should at least tell you if your problem sits with DW or with your app, so suggesting where investigate farther.

            Dear Team,    "react-native-datawedge-intents": "^0.1.8", I hope you're doing well.We are currently using your library in our React Native project to integrate with Zebra DataWedge, and it has been working well on older Android versions. However, after upgrading our devices to Android 13 (API 33), we encountered a critical issue: the app crashes on startup due to missing android:exported attributes in the manifest receivers.This is due to a change in Android 12+ where all exported components (such as BroadcastReceivers with intent-filters)  Unfortunately, it appears that react-native-datawedge-intents has not been updated recently to reflect this requirement. As a result, the library is no longer compatible with Android 13 and prevents our application from functioning on newer devices.
