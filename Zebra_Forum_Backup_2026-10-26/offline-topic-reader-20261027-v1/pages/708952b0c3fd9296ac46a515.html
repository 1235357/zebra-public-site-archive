            Hi Michael, I've documented myself a little bit and what I can say about it is that the scanners should be compatible as long as they're being used as a Keyboard Emulator, meaning that you can't control other functions such as defining the supported symbologies. To do that you must use our proprietary application called DataWedge. But in that case the customer will need to create a custom application either Hybrid (Xamarin for example) or Native (Android)
