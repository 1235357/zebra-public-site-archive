            Two possible things:Do a Power-Off reset of the Reader.  (* if you are logging in via "http://", vs "https://" - that could be a clue the Reader may be running older Firmware. *)Possibly use USB thumbdrive/stick update; to update the firmware.An alternative to using the Web Console - is to use 123RFID Desktop app for Windows 11.  This uses the Reader HOST-BASED RFID SDK to access the reader - and you may get further in knowing the Firmware Version running on the FX7500. ( you also can do the FW update via the "Firmware" sidetab on the app - if you have the Firmware Files/Folder in the appropriate folder on your Windows PC.
