                  Guys,   A customer is seeing issues with the Bluetooth  on the MC7596 (WinMo 6-1 BSP2.35) following deployment of Active Synch Exchange email.  In summary, following deployment of the Active Synch Exchange email, we are unable to connect to a Bluetooth printer (over a serial port COM9). They also note that some of the Stonestreet  Bluetooth settings are changed. This issue only occurs on devices that have Active Synch Exchange email deployed – other devices behave normally. The Exchange Policy explicitly allows Bluetooth.  Anyone seen anything similar?
