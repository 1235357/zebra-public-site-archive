We have fully working solution based on SAP ITS for warehouse operations (including barcode scanning).
It is operated on Zebra MC3300 (Android + EB/SOTI Surf).
Now there is new requirement
1. Scan some barcode
2. Under next button trigger devices camera
3. Take photo
4. Upload that photo back to SAP (ITS, no Fiori)
Does anyone has any materials about similar solutions? Is that possible?

            Yes, it is possible to integrate a solution that scans a barcode, triggers the device's camera, takes a photo, and uploads it to SAP ITS. You can develop a native Android app or use a cross-platform framework like Xamarin or React Native to achieve this.
