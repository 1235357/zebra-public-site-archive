So I suggest that you contact the OEM of your device.   is this an SPR  or do we have an statement that ES400-MC65 is not supporting this .

            Dave consulted IAC&nbsp; and we have found that disabling&nbsp; NITZ on ES400 - MC65 will fix this.   Disable NITZ&nbsp; Time Sync to current time zone&nbsp;  see&nbsp; Clock&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; and disable "Sync to current time zone"&nbsp; and press OK   or use regkey to do this alternatively.
[HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Software\Microsoft\Clock]
&nbsp;
"NetworkTimeSync"=dword:00000000&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; ; default value = 1   if you now do an Activesync and have an partnership&nbsp; then time and date are synchronzed&nbsp; from PC&nbsp; if the option within Activesync on PC has been set to 
&nbsp;
‘synchronize Pocket PC date and time upon connecting’
