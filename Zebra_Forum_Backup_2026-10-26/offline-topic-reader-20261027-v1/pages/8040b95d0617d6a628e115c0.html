            the issue with startup-control is that someone would have to active-sync the device with their PC to install the startup-control tool.   we want to streamline the process so a computer-savvy&nbsp;technician who may not be familiar with WindowsCE would take a unit out of the box insert an MicroSD card and start the process.   The goal is to limit the user's role to be a "simple" mechanical and repeatable process.
