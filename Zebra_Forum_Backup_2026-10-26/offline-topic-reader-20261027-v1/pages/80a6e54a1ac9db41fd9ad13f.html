            David and all..
First, I was informed late last week when I received the newest scanner firmware (fw) update app for the TC75 that you no longer need to disable DataWedge before running the fw update... the latest installers (including the MC40 Rev C one) do that for you.
The non reboot you mention is also most likely a factor in the newest fw installer.
What you are experiencing does seem to be correct behavior and you don't need to reboot.&nbsp; Don't know about running it to check the version without actually installing it again... the team actually recommends after running it the one time, to uninstall it.
I am posting a newer READ ME now (v4) that will update the fw instructions.
Very sorry for the confusion.
