            Chris,  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; We do not have a UAString option in settings to allow the user to&nbsp;flip to desktop mode.    Most mobile pages do offer a view option (normally in an obscure area of the page which allows the user to flip into desktop mode.&nbsp; Normally you do this once for the respective site and any successive view of the site will remain in desktop until you clear cache and history.    You may consider a GRIP for the UAString option in settings.&nbsp; Hopefully when we get to IceCream or Jelly bean (or if we do), the option will be there.    good luck
