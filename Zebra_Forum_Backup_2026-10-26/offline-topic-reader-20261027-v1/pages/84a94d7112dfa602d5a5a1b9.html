            I use this in my StartUpCtlConfig.txt located in \Application\StartUpCtl\ folder.
[Splash_Properties]
ImagePath=\Application\StartUpCtl\PleaseWait.bmp
Then I turn it on in my OnRestore.txt file at the start of my actions.
#
#
SplashOn
delay 10
actions....
