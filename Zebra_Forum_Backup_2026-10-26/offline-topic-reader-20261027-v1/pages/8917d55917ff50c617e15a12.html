            FYI, I have submitted a CPR to create this cable.   if anyone else has a similar customer requirement, please feel free to reference CRP#6872.&nbsp;   our specific case, we need an RS232 DB9 connector at the end of the cable and can work with software flow control.&nbsp; you may need to specify your own specific connector type and signaling requirements.   thanks,  afshin
