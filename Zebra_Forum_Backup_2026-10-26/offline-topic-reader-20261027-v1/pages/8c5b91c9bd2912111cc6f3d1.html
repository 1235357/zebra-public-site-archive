                  Hi Everyone!
The development team is beginning to work on the Bluetooth integration with CS60 and RFD8500 to scan and read tags.  The initial prototype encompassed a text box in the iOS mobile app that would receive tags via the scanner being in the HID mode.  This presents other problems, especially when the iOS device is locked and still receiving hundreds of tags.  
We are looking to pair and receive tag data and have looked at a couple of NPM packages that mention achieving this but wanted to ping the community on the best direction for this project.  There are Datawedge, Bluetooth, and NPM packages so we are looking for the best approach.  We only need tag data and don't want the HID mode.   
thanks!!
