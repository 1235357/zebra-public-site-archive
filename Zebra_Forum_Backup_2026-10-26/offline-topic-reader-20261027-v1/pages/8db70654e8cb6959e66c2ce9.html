            Ok, after much printing seems like there is a bug between the driver and chrome.
In adobe and presumably most other software there is only one paper size.
In chrome there is 'blank' and 'user defined' select blank and all is fine. 'User defined' and you get landscape across 2 stickers.
So solution don't choose 'User defined' . Also chrome seems to remember this kind of stuff (to be helpful) and this caused the problem for me.
