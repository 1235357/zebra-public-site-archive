            I recently looked into this for one of my customers. Silverlight will not run on Windows Mobile 6.X, and there are no plans to make it work on Windows Mobile 6.X.&nbsp; Opera claims to have a way to do it, but I think they are rendering the pages on a&nbsp;server somewhere and sending screen updates to the browser, so you would need a connection to the internet, and I think the web site may also have to be in the internet. I have not tried the Opera solution.   For our customer, we started looking into using an MW810 (full Windows).
