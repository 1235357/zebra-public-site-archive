            I have seen something similiar, in that the terminal will not power on when the power key is pressed, but will wakeup in placed in cradle.&nbsp; To resolve clean the contacts on the bottom of the terminal with some contact cleaner, another symptom of thsi is that you may also start seeing active sync/cradle issues.   At least 2 customers in the UK have seen this issue.&nbsp; There is also a fix in product hot fixes for the 'corrosion issue' which should also be applied.
