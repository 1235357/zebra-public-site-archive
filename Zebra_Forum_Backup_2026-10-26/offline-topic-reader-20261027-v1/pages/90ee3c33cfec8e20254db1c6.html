                  All,
For those of you who are using my Compass site of MCD accessories, I've created a new Compass shortcut link.  The new link for the Compass site that lists pictures and part numbers for all of our MCD devices (and some ADC, CEC, and RFID devices) is now http://compass.mot-solutions.com/go/enterpriseaccessories.&nbsp;  
For those of you not familiar with the site, it lists both Motorola and 3rd party accessories for each device.
The old link (http://compass.mot-solutions.com/go/mobilecomputingaccessories ) still works, but it will only take you to the current MCD devices listing. 
I needed to clean things up a bit because there were too many devices listed which made you have to scroll through multiple pages.  The new site categorizes products by BU and then if they are current or discontinued.
Thanks,
Ken
