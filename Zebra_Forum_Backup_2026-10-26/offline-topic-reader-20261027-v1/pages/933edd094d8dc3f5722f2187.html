            The ZD421 printer support the PDF Direct. You can following this article to enable the PDF Direct on the printer: Announcement | Printers Include Free PDF Direct with Link-OS 6.3
Then open a TCP connection from the PHP application to the printer on the port 9100. Once it's connected, you can send the PDF file directly from the PHP application to the printer over the TCP connection to print the PDF>
