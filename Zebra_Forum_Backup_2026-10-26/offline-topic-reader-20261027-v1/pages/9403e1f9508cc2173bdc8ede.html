            Most likely they may be referring to how fast they can capture the pointing device position.&nbsp; With most signature capture algorithms, you sample the signature while it is being drawn on the screen to capture an array of points that represents the signature.  On our devices,&nbsp; this could be performed as fast as every 10 msec. (or, 100 samples / second).&nbsp; However, remember that the faster sample rate could affect the overall responsiveness of the device.
