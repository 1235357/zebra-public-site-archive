            Try running the ZXP 7 Toolbox as administrator, I've just tried it on my unit here and it displayed the cards readers correctly.
The Elatec reader is fitted to the new ZC range of card printers, the ZXP series has the SCM Microsystems / Identive SDI 010  instead.
