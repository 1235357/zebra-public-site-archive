            Hi Rick,This is due to the wireless module has been disabled in the printer. Use ZSU app to enable the same.In ZSU app, once you connect via BT, goto connectivity Settings and the wireless and enable the toggle button, add relevant data like SSID password and apply, it will ask for printer reboot. Once done, you should be able to connect printer via wireless as well.
