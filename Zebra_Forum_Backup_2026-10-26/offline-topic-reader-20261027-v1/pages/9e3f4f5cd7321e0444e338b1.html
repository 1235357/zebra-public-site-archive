            Ron,   I just checked with my contact at Vocollect.&nbsp; Here's his response:    Here is the current list of supported devices and VoiceClient versions:  
MC9090 (only models with audio connectors) – VoiceClient for Motorola version 1.3 (we didn’t update this after the MC9500 was released)
WT4000 (all models) – VoiceClient for Motorola version 2.0
MC9500 – VoiceClient for Motorola version 2.0 – SRX Wireless Headset Only&nbsp; (this is dev/qa complete but we haven’t released it officially for commercial reasons, but we would release it to a customer who is interested)
  Other devices we are targeting for support in the next year:  
MC9190 (and any rumored updates to this)
MC3190
WT41N0
