Specifically about our setup: we are using the Sipek C# API (http://voipengine.googlepages.com/), which is a C#/.NET wrapper for the much-used and extensively tested pjsip SIP stack (http://www.pjsip.org/). When I press the VQM icon in the top bar, is see that AEC is off. We didn't switch it off from the software, and as you know you can't set or unset AEC from anywhere in the settings manually.
From VQM UG :
The Audio Echo Cancellation(AEC) mode assumes importance only during voice transmission.Also the device consumes more power in AEC Mode.So , the device enters the w/AEC modes only during an Active Voice Call.Prior to the call, if the device was in Speaker mode it transitions to the Speaker w/AEC mode and if the device was in Handset mode it transitions to the Handset w/AEC mode.For the duration of the call consecutive taps shall toggle the device between Handset w/AEC mode and Speaker w/AEC mode.The user has no control over turning on and turning off of Echo Cancellation during an active voice calls.
