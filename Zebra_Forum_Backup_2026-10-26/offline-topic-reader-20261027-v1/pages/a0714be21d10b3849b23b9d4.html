            We use Certs on Android, Time and Date is set via an MDM. &nbsp;Users have Zero access to configure time and date without the MDM so if it gets out of whack the only recourse is to send to depot. &nbsp;Even further, if the device has not contacted the MDM for 6 consecutive hours it will auto wipe, that is the device unable to connect for 6 hours of on time.
