                  Problem is that Zebra.Printer.SDK 4.0.3435 has a dependency on SkiaSharp.Views.Maui.Controls which at some point in the dependency chain pulls in the Maui source generator and that conflicts with the WinUI source generator, breaking the project. Maybe this is intentional, but it seems like an odd dependency to have. Workaround is rolling back to 3.0.3355
