            You can use Jpos (Java POS) which is part of UPOS (Unified POS)  standards that is supported by different vendors .

Zebra provided JPOS support for both windows and Linux .

Documentation can be found under 

https://techdocs.zebra.com/dcs/scanners/jpos-driver/about/
