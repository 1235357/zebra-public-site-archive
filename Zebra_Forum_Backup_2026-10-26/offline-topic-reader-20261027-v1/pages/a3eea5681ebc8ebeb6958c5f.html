            Don,  Check out HID.  https://www.hidglobal.com/products/rfid-tags/identification-technologie…   They have a keg tag that worked great in some recent testing. For 16 gal kegs, we were reading 8 kegs per pallet, 3 pallets at a time (24 tags)&nbsp;with a fork truck going through a portal at normal speed.   We read 30 of the smaller kegs in one pass through the portal.   Portal was from New Wave Sensors.
