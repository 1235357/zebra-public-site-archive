            The A13 release for a majority of the SD660 devices is now here, as of July 25th. There are several SD660 devices like the MC9300 and VC8300 that don't have available updates yet but are still scheduled for later this year. There are other SD660 devices that won't be getting A13 at all. The recent PMB has the most up to date information on the release schedule and impacted devices.
