            Hi Tariq  I have found the attached competitive sheet on Compass, so hope this will be of use.
Would be interested to see how the TFT displays copes with indoor and outdoor use as we use transflective so that LED backlighting illuminates the display indoors, but also the reflective surface produces readable images outdoors.
From tests in the UK for parking enforcement, with all radios switched on including GPS, I saw battery improvement anywhere between 1.5 to 2 hours using our 4800mah battery compared to their 4000mah battery.
For IP64, what type of area are the devices to be used? For dust protection, 5 indicates ingress of dust is not entirely prevented, but it must not enter in sufficient quantity to interfere with the satisfactory operation of the equipment; complete protection against contact.
