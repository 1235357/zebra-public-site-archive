            Out of topic but... 
 Alternative PTT software you can look into is Mobile Tornado and GroupTalk. 
  G4S is deploying right now MC65 &amp; ES400 with Mobile Tornado PTT but has been using it for while on MC70, MC75 &amp; MC55. They seems to be happy with solution as they are now replacing their old school walkie-talkies with our devices... 
  What's impressive with Mobile Tornado is that it seems to work on a GPRS/EDGE network (low bandwidth / high latency).&nbsp;Otherwise 3G (HSDPA) or better capacity is recommended for this kind of solutions and I'm not sure how CDMA works with PTT.
