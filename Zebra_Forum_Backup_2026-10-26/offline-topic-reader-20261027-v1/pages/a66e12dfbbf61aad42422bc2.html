            It looks like the text and the logo were sent to the printer in two different ZPL scripts. A ZPL script is defined as a sequence of ZPL commands enclosed within a pair of ^XA&nbsp;and ^XZ for one label. Therefore, the text and the logo were printed out as if they were two separate labels. To make them as the integral parts of one label, you need to put the logo image data into the ZPL script for the text. The easiest way is to use ZebraDesigner for Developers 3 tool to design a label template.
