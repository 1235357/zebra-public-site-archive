            The label size involves the width and length of a label. The length of a label can be detected through the media calibration. The width of the label is predetermined through the type of printers and width of the media, for example, 2" printer vs 4" printer. The calibration process doesn't auto detect the width of a label. Once the media in the printer has been calibrated, the program can query the length of the label
! U1 getvar "zpl.label_length"
Please note, printers accepts the media that is narrower than the printer width.
