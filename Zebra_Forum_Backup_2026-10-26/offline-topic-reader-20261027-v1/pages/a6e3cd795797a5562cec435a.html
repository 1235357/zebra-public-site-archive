            Hello,  Unfortunately yes. We faced with this on two customers. One is about more than 100 devices were having duplicate UUID numbers with MC75 and other is about 10 pieces of MC5574 devices were in the same situation.   Problem is coming from the memory/processor chip itself and no way to reflash/ rebuild etc.  you may search for it on SPR #18919   The only way is replacing devices or convincing customer to use another reference instead of UUID.  There is nothing to do on MSP to avoid conflicts.   I hope it helps at least to understand.
