            Hi Markus, normally you'll not have the phone number stored in the SIM card, so it's not possible to recover it.  To workaround this, what we have done sometimes, is create a "special" contact in the SIM card with your own phone number, so the applicatión could search for that contact and&nbsp;find the number.
