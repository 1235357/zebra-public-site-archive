                  Team,   Does RDP client included with WM6.5 or CE6 support the RemoteApp mode (introduced in Windows Server 2008 R2)? The RemoteApp mode means that the client shows only the application window and not the entire remore desktop space thus the remote application looks and feels like being executed locally. More here:   http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_Desktop_Services#RemoteApp   Thanks,  Valery
