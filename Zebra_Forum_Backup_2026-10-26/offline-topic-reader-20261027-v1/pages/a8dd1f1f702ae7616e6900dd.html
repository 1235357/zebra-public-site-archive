            FYI - An APK had to be created (from Support) to back off the firmware upgrade.&nbsp; FullPackageUpdate got rid of the FirmwareUpgrade app installed but the firmware itself persisted even through Factory Reset.&nbsp; The APK with older firmware return the device to "out of box" version.
