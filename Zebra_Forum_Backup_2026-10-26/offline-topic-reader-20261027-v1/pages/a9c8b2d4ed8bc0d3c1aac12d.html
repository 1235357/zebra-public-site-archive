            I agree with your suspicion.&nbsp; It all depends upon how discharged the batteries are.&nbsp; There comes a point where the battery is so depleted, that the current required to initiate charging doesn't leave any extra current to wake the terminal.&nbsp; (i.e. there isn't enough power to trigger the interrupts required to wakeup the device)
