            Ian,   I have a customer using the MC95 that just upgraded to BSP 41 from BSP 27 and they are also reporting that the volume level is too low with BSP 41...even with the volume slider set to its highest level.&nbsp; I was told by engineering that prior BSPs had the volume output set too high which could cause damage to the speaker.&nbsp; I was also told there is no REG key we can apply to revert the volume level back to what BSP27/BSP37 used.&nbsp; My customer will be reverting back to BSP 37 so they get the louder output.&nbsp; They haven't had any speaker failures.&nbsp;   I think engineering may have been too aggressive with lowering the volume output on BSP41.   Thanks,  Ken
