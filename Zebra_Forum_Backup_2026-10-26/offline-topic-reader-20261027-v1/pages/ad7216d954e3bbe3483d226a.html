                  I need to deploy Browser Print using Microsoft Intune / Endpoint Manager to our Android devices.  We use android enterprise vs Device Administrator, which requires us to deploy our applications via our Managed Google Play.  I tried to upload the browser print apk into our managed google play (since it's not available in the public store,) but it failed since it's a registered app with google play - the application ID is not unique. What is the recommended method of deployment via MDM when you can't sideload apk?

            As you have already noticed, the Browser Print Android app has not been published on the public Google Play. However, some other customers probably have already uploaded the app on their managed Google Play. Therefore the app cannot be uploaded again to the managed Google Play because the ID is not unique. The best way to address the issue is to contact your account manager from Zebra and ask the account manager to make a CAG ZAP request for a special build of the Browser Print Android app with a unique ID, so that it can be uploaded to the managed Google Play.

            The CAG stands for Customer Application Group, a team in Zebra in charge of printer software customization. The ZAP stands for Zebra Applications Process, an internal process that captures the specific customizations.
