            One solution used by a customer is to enable the SIM PIN, then have some software submit the PIN automatically - so the user is not prompted&nbsp; - this is easy to do (SymScript has a command for this) using the SIM API. - The user is not aware of the SIM PIN.  But should they remove and put in another device they will be prompted for the SIM PIN - at which point they must be very lucky or they lock the SIM requiring the PUK code.
