                  Hello All,
Here are the notes from our call today.  Please be advised that the next RFID SEVT call will be held on 08/05.  Please review the notes and action items.  Engage with the SEVT team on SE Zone at: http://bit.ly/rfidsevt
 Call Agenda – 07/01/2015
Attendance – listed below
RFD8500 review
SEVT Summit
Round table
 Notes
RFD8500 (GenX) – Altaf Mulla
UHF sled which has RFID and barcode options – with imager and without imager
8500 is multi-platform – Android and iOS – windows in 2016 as needed based on devices. iOS support for v8.0
Timing: beta is going to start July 10th – waiting on software bug resolution – testing in-process with Target
Launch: Planned date of Sept 9th FCS shifted to October
Features for beta – barcode and scanning functionality – batch processing might not be available
Sled is best performing in its class – very high performance, battery life (5-6 hrs).
Accessories in plan – adapter for MC40 & TC55 – TC70 is not planned out of the gate but for post Rev A in Q1, 2016 – with TC70 the total solution would be a 2 piece solution due to weight of devices
Demo apps both for iOS and Android will help in testing for locationing, tags etc – beta will use the demo apps and any partner apps (beta partners included as part of extended development team)
Beta partners – 18-20 worldwide – specifically which customers who are working with RFID and partners currently engaged with those opportunities – feedback required by end of August.
8500 is completely build from ground up – TSL is a key competitor – competitive analysis will be released.  Performance is 500 tags per second for 8500 while TSL is 180 tags/second.  Higher acquisition range compared to TSL.  Battery capacity is 2x better for Zebra product. We developed our own radio called NGE – provides optimizations based on environment.
8500 support ASCII interface to work on multiple OS/devices.  No focus towards Windows yet.  In future a consideration would be taken based on Windows use cases.  Device supports HID mode.
For older Windows Zebra devices 5500 would work.
There is a iOS and Android SDK for 8500 being build.  Both will be available at Launch.  No Windows SDK at this point.
Multi charging options for 8500 – 3 slot cradle to charge – micro USB and a cup cable. Same battery as TC55 – able to use a lot of the common accessories as TC55.  TC55 + 8500 becomes a great combination with accessories. (key highlight point)
SEVT Summit – RFID
Helpful and needed interaction – both regional and global.
EMEA feeling is that lot of RFID stuff is focused towards NA
Combine with DCS summit – do this event late September –  in Rockville
Action: Please let Ritesh and Larry know about topics of interest for summit here: http://bit.ly/rfidsevt
Round table discussion
- No plans to create a new fixed reader product – no support for RS232.  Use case is towards a fork-lift reader.  Solution might be a Bluetooth dongle.
- List of validated Bluetooth dongles – Altaf to follow up
- Show a how-to document for getting notifications from SEVT and SE zone (Action: Ritesh / Larry)
