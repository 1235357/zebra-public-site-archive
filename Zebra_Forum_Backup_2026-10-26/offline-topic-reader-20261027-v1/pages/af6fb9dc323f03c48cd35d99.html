            For security reasons, iOS doesn't allow its apps to search or discover the Bluetooth Classic devices, unless they are Bluetooth Low Energy devices. For Bluetooth Classic printers, a user  has to first pair and connect to a printer via the iOS device's Bluetooth setting. It could be that the iOS 16 device has not paired with the ZD420 before, therefore, the discovery method cannot detect the printer. Try to pair with the ZD420 printer first, then run the app to call the discovery method.
