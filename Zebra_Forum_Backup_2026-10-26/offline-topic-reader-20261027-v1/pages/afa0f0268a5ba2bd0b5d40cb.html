            This is behaviour of Internet Explorer in Win CE7.
You can use one of the following solutions.
1.Try on Webkit version of EnterpriseBrowser. You will not see the issue there.
Or
2. Set the following registry to change the IE behaviour.
[HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Internet Explorer\EditActivation] "EnableEditActivation"=dword:00000000
