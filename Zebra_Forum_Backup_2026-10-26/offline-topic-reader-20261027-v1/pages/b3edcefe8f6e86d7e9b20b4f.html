            Mike, I would suggest that you use the Rho Shared Runtime (browser/rendering engine).&nbsp; We have a customer that is using SAP ITS and have leveraged the Rho engine over Wavelink.&nbsp; A few things to condiser with ITS - HTML5 support in Rho that that WL does not provide.        You may have to tweak the "UserAgent" in the config.xml file (the webservice we were hitting had a white list).
