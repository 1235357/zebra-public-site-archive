            Hi Ian/Venky,  Please reference the link below for proper adjustment of volume settings on the MC17 http://msdn.microsoft.com/en-us/library/ms940081.aspx  For example, creating a registry file(.REG extension placed in \Application) with the following entries below disables sounds of all key clicks and screen taps, and enables only application sounds: : [HKEY_CURRENT_USER\ControlPanel\Volume] "Mute"=dword:00000002 "Key"=dword:00000000 "Volume"=dword:55555555 "Screen"=dword:00000000  Regards, Tim
