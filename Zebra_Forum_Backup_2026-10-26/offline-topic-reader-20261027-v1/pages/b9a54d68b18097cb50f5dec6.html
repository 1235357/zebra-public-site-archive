                  Hi All, I have a large customer with a MC7090 (WM Premier 5 OS) who has a Softphone app running on it.  However, they also have implemented the WM device lock OS feature to lock the device after 5 minutes of non-use.  After the device locks an incoming call cannot be answered until the user enters the unlock code.  Can anyone help provide direction on a method to define / code the app to be allowed to run and let the user answer the Softphone call without unlocking the WM OS - similar to the way dialers on WM Smartphone work?  Thank you in advance for your help!

            Based on the information in your post, one solution may be to code a custom LAP (Local Athentication Protocol) plugin for the customer.  A LAP is the underlying technology that is used when&nbsp;the user needs to unlock the device by entering a password.&nbsp; It's possible to implement a custom LAP&nbsp;and override the default behavior to "automatically" unlock the device&nbsp;when an incoming phone call is pending (as long as the incoming call be be detected).  I've had to implement a custom LAP for another customer to override the default lock behavior.&nbsp; For this customer, the security protocol was enabled by the Exchange Server.&nbsp; They wanted our devices to not be locked while standard PDA's would be authenticated.  If you want to include me on a conference call with the customer to discuss this approach, contact me directly.&nbsp; This is the sort of work my group at Motorola performs under the aegis of ECRT.
