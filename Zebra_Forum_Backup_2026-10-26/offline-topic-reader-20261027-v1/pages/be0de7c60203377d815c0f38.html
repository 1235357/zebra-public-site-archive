            Dan, you can do the following.
Get old BSP (.zip file) on your PC, same directory as adb
Connect your device to the PC, start a terminal window, move to folder where adb is located.
On your device scroll down, select apply update over adb
On PC terminal window, type adb devices, check you device is displayed
On PC terminal window type adb sideload
