            The starting point for developers is the Printer Support portal itself so start here:https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/printers/card/zc350.htmlFrom there you will find the SDK portal for the support:https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/printer-software/developer-tools/card-sdk.htmlAs you will see from that series of Links, the Link-OS Multiplatform SDK Support - indicates that this supports the ZC350 platform.Please note: This is only the beginning - since Card Printing needs a lot more support / setup than what I am indicating here.( Zebra Designer,  Methods for sending data to the printer that use templating, and other features. )
