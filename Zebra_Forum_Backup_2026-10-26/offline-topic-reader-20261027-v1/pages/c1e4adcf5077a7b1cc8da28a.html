            Hi, yes, you can access the Zebra Scanner from a Xamarin.Forms app and the recommended approach for this is to use DataWedge.&nbsp; The best resources for this are:
-&nbsp;https://pietromaggi.com/2018/07/04/xamarin.forms-freshmvvm-for-enterpris...
- https://pietromaggi.com/2018/07/07/xamarin.forms-freshmvvm-datawedge/
-&nbsp;https://pietromaggi.com/2018/07/11/xamarin.forms-freshmvvm-datawedge-tak...
You would also need some logic to test the device manufacturer and if(Zebra) then (use DataWedge).
The above articles will apply to all of the device models you have listed.
