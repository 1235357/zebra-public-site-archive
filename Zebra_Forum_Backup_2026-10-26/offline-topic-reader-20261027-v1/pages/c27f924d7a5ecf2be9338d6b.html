            Howard,  &nbsp;This could be due to the GPRS session not being re-established when the unit goes back into coverage i.e. the AS client does not automatically connect because there is no current GPRS connection whereas the session is being reconnected when the manual sync process is initiated. If this is the case then you may be able to avoid it by setting the GPRS always on registry key as detailed in the article posted at http://forum.xda-developers.com/showthread.php?t=273450  Best Regards, Ian Hatton EMb EMEA
