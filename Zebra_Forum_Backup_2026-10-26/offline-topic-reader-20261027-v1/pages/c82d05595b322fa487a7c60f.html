                  Hi,  BSP 26 for MC3190 Windows CE is built in with Wavelink Telnet CE 7.0.76. The Active Sync Wavelink software to transfer host and emulation parameters works OK (I used 7.2.4 version) but the backup / restore mechanism on cold boot is not implemented. Persistence is not correctly implemented.  Solution is solved with upgrade to Telnet CE 7.0.103.  Thank you to Nicolas and Petr from EMEA support centre for their quick support.   Regards,  David
