            Hi Sean, I took a bit of time before replying in order to finish testing. The "State Aware" topic is clear to me, thanks anyway for the clarification. The purpose of my question was to verify the FX9600's performance in IoT+MQTT mode and understand why, with certain configurations, I was getting few reads per second even in S0. I now think I've found confirmation of the hypothesis made in the post above. Using target AB, I get good throughput. Using target A with a single antenna, I get very few reads per second. Adding a second antenna, the read rate increases, and in particular it's affected by the duration of the antennaStopCondition parameter. I think this confirms that with a single antenna, the tag stays powered continuously and switches slowly from state B back to A. With two antennas, the tag loses power at every antenna switch, returning to state A.
