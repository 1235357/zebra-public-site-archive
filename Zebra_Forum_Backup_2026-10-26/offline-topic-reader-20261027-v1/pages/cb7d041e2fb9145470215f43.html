            For card printer, there is an SDK for card printers in PC-Java. You can check out the documentation at&nbsp;https://techdocs.zebra.com/link-os/2-14/pc_card/. There is a UsbConnection class that can be used to talk to the card printer over the USB. Regarding to "com.zebra.sdk.comm.DriverPrinterConnection", I think is meant for label printers.
