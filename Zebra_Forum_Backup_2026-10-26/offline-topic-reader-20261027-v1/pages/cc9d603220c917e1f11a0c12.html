            Mike,  The walkie talkie connector on the bottom of the unit provides speaker/headset output.&nbsp; Sinbon provides the cable which connects to a push-to-talk handset.&nbsp; The handset has an integrated speaker.&nbsp; In addition, the handset also has a jack for a corded headset.&nbsp; You may be able to plug speakers into this jack or just use the speaker that's built into the handset.&nbsp; See http://compass.mot.com/go/mobilecomputingaccessories&nbsp;for P/N and ordering info.  Thanks, Ken
