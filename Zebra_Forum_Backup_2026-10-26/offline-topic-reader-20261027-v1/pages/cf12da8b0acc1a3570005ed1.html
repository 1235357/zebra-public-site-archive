            Continues scanning is not available on Android devices.&nbsp; The way you code around this is to use DW Intents, Android EMDK, RhoMobile, or Enterprise Browser by starting the scanning and setting the timeout value to zero so that the scanner stays on until a barcode is read.&nbsp; Once a barcode is read then the application will start the scanning again via a command to the scanner to start scanning.
I done what you are looking for with all of the above options and in the end comes down to what tools you are comfortable in using.
I attached two samples that you should show you how its done.
