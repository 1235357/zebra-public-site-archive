            This reg setting should make the screen render like the older versions of Pocket IE. &nbsp;Does not necessarily change the size, but does remove the zoom bar, etc.   [HKEY_LOCAL_MACHINE\Security\Internet Explorer]  "MSHTML"=dword:00000000
