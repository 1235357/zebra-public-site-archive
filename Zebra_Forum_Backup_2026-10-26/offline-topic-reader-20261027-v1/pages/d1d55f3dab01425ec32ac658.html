            Daniel is partly correct:

https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/rfid/rfid-handhelds/mc3300xr.html 
Is the support portal for MC33x0XR RFID Platforms.

The Developer details is further down in this Support Portal page.

The MC33 - like the RFD40 STD Sled with a Zebra Android Device connected in the Sled Carrie via eConnex - uses Datawedge for the Barcode system. ( The trigger to make the barcode scanner turn on - is the "Terminal Scanner" menu choice - when setting the upper or lower trigger.
RFD devices - triggers default to "Sled Trigger" for the RFD40 and RFD90 sleds by default.

 

Quick Point: EMDK for Barcode - is actually suggested to "Not Use This" but to use Datawedge instead.

Similarly - the EMDK support for RFID - is no longer supported (like it was for Little Windows Symbol RFID platforms.)



But the respective RFID SDK for a given Development Environment is found in the MC33 RFID Support portal.

Also if you want to understand the Mobile RFID - on iOS or on Android - the 123RFID Mobile app is very good.
This app is for Android - an Android Studio - developed app.

If you use the RFD40 Premium Plus, or RFD90, or RFD8500 mobile sleds with Integrated Barcode Scanner on a "NON-Zebra Android" platform (like Google Pixel "google reference platform") or on iOS  - the 123RFID Mobile app uses the Scanner Framework SDK to access the Barcode scanner..
