    From my understanding, the screen density reported by Motorola ET1 is incorrect, while dpi means “Dot Per Inch”. For screen density of 240 dpi with resolution 1024 X 600, the screen size should be (1024/240)” X (600/240)” = 4.27” X 2.5” which is a normal 5” screen, instead of 7”. Therefore, the system display their application’s layout incorrectly.
    
Similar problem did occurred in Samsung Galaxy Tab (7”).
