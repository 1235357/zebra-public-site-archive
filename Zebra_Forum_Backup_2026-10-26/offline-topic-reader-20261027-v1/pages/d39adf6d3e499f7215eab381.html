            Hey Afshin,  I am not seeing this issue but never under-estimate the importance of port settings. I've seen many issues in the past when settings are not correct:  1. Port Speed and Duplex - check wired port settings to match our switch uplink ports, AP300's should be Auto. 2. MSTP enabled by default, not compatible with Cisco PVST so issues all over with this one!  When not set properly, load balancing across cluster has issues, 'a' radios adopt and then unadopt while the b/g radio on the same AP&nbsp;remains fine etc...some strange stuff happens!
