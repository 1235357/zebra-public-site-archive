            Hi Manohar - refer to my Devcon2023 presentation deck here https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/zebra_dam/en/presentation/customer-fa…
Slides #25 and #26 cover an Ionic project based on Angular to gain access to a Zebra device's resources through Enterprise Browser.
The recording is found here https://www.zebra.com/content/dam/zebra_dam/en/video/web-production/zeb…
Enterprise Browser (EB) is defined here https://www.zebra.com/gb/en/software/mobile-computer-software/enterpris… and is a licensed browser from Zebra.
In the above example, I used the EB APIs to capture Android Intents generated by the scanning framework (which is configured via Datawedge);but you can use this API to get the device's MAC Address https://techdocs.zebra.com/enterprise-browser/3-7/api/signalindicators/…
