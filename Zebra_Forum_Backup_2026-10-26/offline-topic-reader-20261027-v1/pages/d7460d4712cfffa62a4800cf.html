            Hi, I am afraid the order that multi-barcodes are decoded is non-deterministic.&nbsp; You would need to scan them individually to be certain of the order (probably using picklist mode to ensure you are scanning the correct one) or you could use some logic around barcode length, symbology or data format to distinguish between the barcodes (though in the attached sample.png it looks as though it may be challenging to distinguish between them).
