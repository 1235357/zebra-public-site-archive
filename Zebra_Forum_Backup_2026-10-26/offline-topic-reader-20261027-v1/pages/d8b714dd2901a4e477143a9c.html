            Hi Nutra.Out-of-the-Box the RS5100 when used with a Zebra Android Device can have some settings adjusted - but not to the questions you have above. Since the RS5100 - when reset to factory defaults - will revert to the same Out-Of-The-Box behavior.There is less that you can do programmaitcally as a result.  Which means you would need to engage Zebra in investigating a Custom Product Request to address your concerns - but this is not free - you will be provided with a Quote for these costs to implement a strategy such as this.
