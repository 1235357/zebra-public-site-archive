            Have you loaded abup30 and enable30 to the devices?  Even with the latest OS Update, RD PEAP profiles will not be interpreted properly from the barcode scan without at least abup30 applied.&nbsp; This will move the RD client version to a version 5.x  I do not have an MC3090 to confirm, but last week I was faced with this challenge on an MC17 with the latest OS Update and PEAP.&nbsp; RD would work as long as the wireless configuration was simple WEP.&nbsp; Once PEAP was selected (no certificates or username/password) the barcode scan process would fail to create the PEAP based profile.  After applying abup30, the exact same barcode staging profile that previously failed -worked.
