            Hi Erik-
In Enterprise Browser, this is done using the HideSystemBar parameter in your app's Config.XML file. This feature requires MX 7.1 or later on the device. Find the docs here: 
https://techdocs.zebra.com/enterprise-browser/3-3/guide/configreference…
Best, 
Eddie C.
