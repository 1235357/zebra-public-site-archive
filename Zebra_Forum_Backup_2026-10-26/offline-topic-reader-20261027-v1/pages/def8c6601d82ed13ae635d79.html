            The ZIOTC - is a component on the FX-Series readers on the Newer Firmware platform.

If your FX-Series reader is not running the up-to-date Firmware, the ZIOTC module may not show up in the Applications List on the Web Console for the Reader.

Verify that the FX-Series reader has been update dot at least V3.21.21 or higher.

FX-Series support portal for FX9600 : https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/rfid/rfid-readers/fx9600.html

FX-Series support portal for FX7500 : https://www.zebra.com/us/en/support-downloads/rfid/rfid-readers/fx7500.html

In Each portal is the Firmware support that you can consult.  
Additionally if you are running - up-to-date - you may need to "Factory Reset" or "Enterprise Reset" the FX-Series reader to allow the IoT config / settings to be setup as expected.
