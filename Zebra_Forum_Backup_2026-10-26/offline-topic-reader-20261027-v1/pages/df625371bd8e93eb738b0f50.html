            I believe it shust off as part of the critical suspend. If customers are seeing it shut down prior to that it sounds possible that they may have went into a critical suspend, then cradled the device to give it just enough power to come out and then to go back in. I agree this is a guess but this does may be a likely scenario. It also could be related to the SPR someone mentioned earlier. Either way, I would suggest gathering some monitoring logs to see if we can pick up any cases where the radio is shutting down prior to the suspend.   Hope this helps.
