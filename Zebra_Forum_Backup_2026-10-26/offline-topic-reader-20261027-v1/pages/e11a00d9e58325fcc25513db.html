            Your problem in the air-watch agent and MX.&nbsp; You cant use the market/play store versions.&nbsp; You need to load the android rugged client version I listed in my previous post.&nbsp; I am 99.9% positive the version of AW you listed will not work.&nbsp; The MX agent it downloads is pre Android "M", and many changes were made to run AW client on Android M.
You ( or your partner or customer) will need to have access to the AW console where this device is to be managed.
You can use the sideload or the RD method.&nbsp; You create the staging enrollment profile in the console:
Devices&gt;Staging &amp; Provisioning&gt;Staging&gt;Add Staging
