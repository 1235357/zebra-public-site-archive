            The memory on this device looks good. It's possible that the problem is resolved before emscript does the next snap. If they can reproduce the issue, try getting a snapshot (or leaving it alone for 30 mins so emscript can auto capture a dump). I suspect it is most likely something to do with the synch contents&nbsp;more so than device space.   I also did a quick google search and found the following url. For this error code, they seem to imply the size of the contents being synched may be a factor. If that is the case, try synching the items in parts to see if there is a specific&nbsp;item causing the issue.   http://www.pocketpcfaq.com/faqs/activesync/exchange_errors.php
