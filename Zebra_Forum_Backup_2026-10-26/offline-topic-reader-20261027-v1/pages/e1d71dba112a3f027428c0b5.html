            Paul,  &nbsp; I would not expect to see a significant difference in performance between two CE units based on memory size although the type of flash chips used in each design could be relevant if they are writing data to on-board flash memory. This could be checked with a simple file benchmark e.g. write and read 100 x 10KB files and record the overall time. Looking at the specs of the MX7, a more significant advantage&nbsp;for&nbsp;the 9090 is the processor spec &nbsp;i.e.  MX7:&nbsp; Intel ® XScale® PXA255 400MHzMC9090: Intel XScale Bulverde PXA270 624 MHz&nbsp; I would expect to see better overall performance with the same application running on the 9090 based on the newer processor + higher clock speed.Best Regards,Ian HattonEMb EMEA
