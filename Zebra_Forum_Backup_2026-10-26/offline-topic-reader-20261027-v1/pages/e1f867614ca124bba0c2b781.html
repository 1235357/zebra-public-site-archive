                  I'm pairing a DS3578-ER to the VC80 and noticed that after scanning the pairing barcode on the VC80, a "Configuring BT Services" window pops up on the screen and never seems to go away.
Here's how to reproduce:
1.  Go into Bluetooth settings and add an Incoming COM port.  It should assign as COM3
2.  Go into TekWedge and have it use COM3
3.  Scan the Serial Port Profile Master barcode - see page 3-13 of the VH10 Manual for this barcode - http://atgsupportcentral.motorolasolutions.com/content/emb/docs/manuals…
4.  Scan the pairing barcode on the VC80.
5.  The VC80 will ask to confirm if the BT device can connect.  Click on the dialog bubble to confirm.
6.  The "Configuring BT Services" window will pop up and will not dismiss (see screen shot below)
