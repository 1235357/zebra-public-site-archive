            Only the RFID SDK - developed apps provide this feature at this time. 

FYI - Datawedge RFID Plug-in does not give you what you seek  (RSSI value shown with an EPC / TAG read)

Your question is related to the TechDoc for same:

https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/13-0/guide/input/rfid/

(Look under Post Filter ) The Datawedge RFID Plug-in only captures Readable Tag data only.

I tested these features on A10 on MC3330XR - and was not satisfied with the result in both DWDemo and RWDemo.

( The results are not intuitive )  Techdocs do not elaborate on this.
