            I believe the following cable may be of help   25-136283-01R   This is RS232 cable for MC55 (same story - no native RS232) and it's based on FTDI chip. As far as know the latest A1 OS for MC65/ES400 has the FDTI driver inside so looks like it should work on MC65 as well.
