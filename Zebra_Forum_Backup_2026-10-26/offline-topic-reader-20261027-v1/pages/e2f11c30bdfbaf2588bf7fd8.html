Has anyone else come accross this or does anyone understand how they can offer so much more additional memory on their device.   This is a very important item in Intermecs favor to our customer.             Marc,   &nbsp; The additional free memory on the CN50 is mainly due to the lack of Intermec add-on applications and a different Qualcomm chipset. We can get closer to their 150MB free memory by disabling all the Moto add-on's such as the Home screen and the dialler and switching to the Reduced Graphics driver which is included in the MC65 A1 OS release. The attached installer will make these configuration changes automatically using REG files and will increase free Program memory (on the MC65 A1 OS only - do not use on Rev A) from 68MB after a cleanboot to 103MB after optimisation.   Best Regards,  Ian Hatton  EMEA
