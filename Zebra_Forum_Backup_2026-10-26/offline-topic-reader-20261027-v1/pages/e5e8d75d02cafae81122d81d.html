            The ds6878 scanner is the same as the ds9808 and the bt connection is the same as the ls4278.&nbsp; With that said you can use 123scan2 to build barcodes that can configure a ds6878 scanner using the ds9808 configs for defining the scanning parm and the ls4278 for defining the bt communication parms.&nbsp; If you need more, there is a beta of the ds6878 for 123scan2.
