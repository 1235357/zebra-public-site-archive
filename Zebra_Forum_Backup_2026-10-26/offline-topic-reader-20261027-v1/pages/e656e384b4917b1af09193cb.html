            John,
The Android keyboard shortcut for browser refresh is Menu+R so it is possible to remap the programmable keys (P1 to P3) to do this e.g. in Settings/Key Programmer , select P2 and remap to Menu and then select P3 and remap to R. After doing this, you should be able to force a page refresh by pressing P2 and then P3 (menu + R)
