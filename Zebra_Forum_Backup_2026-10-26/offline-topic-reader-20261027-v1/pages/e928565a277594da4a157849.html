            It's generally not possible to run an Android 7 app directly on a device running Android 13 due to compatibility issues and changes in the Android operating system across different versions. However, you may be able to use compatibility modes or virtualization techniques to run older apps on newer Android versions, but it's not guaranteed to work perfectly or be supported by all apps. It's recommended to check with the app developer or Zebra support for any compatibility options or solutions specific to your device and app.But you did help me a bit by mentioning this https://developer.zebra.com/content/android-emulator-0sexdolltech, and I thank you very much for that.
