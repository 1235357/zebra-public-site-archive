            MC70 GPS unit will have Release 3 modem check under Phone Info&nbsp; 3.010&nbsp; modem version  To resolve no sound on Phone calls you definetly need the RIL 1.1.2.79 from Betazone on top of OEM 5.43.
For Release 3 modems we have since JUNE 2008 a beta RIL available version 1.1.2.79.
See http://devzone.symbol.com/betazone.cfm? under MC70
MC 70X4 - WM 5.0 - MC70X4 with Rel3 modem and OEM 5.43.01 or older disables voice call tone and audio if a call is made from the device - SPR 15300
