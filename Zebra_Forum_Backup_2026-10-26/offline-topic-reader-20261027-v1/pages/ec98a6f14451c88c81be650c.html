            They have created a new Intermediate cert and got it installed. The problem is that you can only have one cert per fusion profile. Thus they must create a new profile for the new cert. Creating a second profile throughout their organization is a huge task, plus you then have to remove the old profile. 
This is our understanding of what needs to be done to implement a new cert. If anyone knows of another way to implement a new cert, please let me know.
