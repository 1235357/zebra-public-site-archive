            To change settings in Admin mode you need to ensure that the Settings package (com.android.settings) is not checked on the 'disable any app' list in Preferences and also that 'System settings restricted' is set to Full Settings. 
To add the ability for the user to select specific system settings such as bluetooth,  you can follow the guide in the blog I posted here: Enterprise Home Screen | Creating Settings Shortcuts | Developer Portal (zebra.com)
