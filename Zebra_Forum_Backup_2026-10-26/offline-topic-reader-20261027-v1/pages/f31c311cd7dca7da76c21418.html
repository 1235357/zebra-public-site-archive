                  All,  Just thinking out loud here, I don't have a customer asking for this functionality or even any idea whether it would work but thought I'd throw it out there and see what you thought.  I was following a truck home the other day which had a reversing camera on the back and I thought to myself, wouldn't it be great if we could connect a reversing camera into the cradle of the MC75 so the driver just had one screen for his sat nav, reversing image, delivery application etc.  And then I thought well how about putting a camera on the front as well and then you could use the MC75 as a sort of "Black Box" for the truck, a camera on the front, one on the back, both recording video with to a large (8GB) SD card in the MC75, great for insurance purposes.  Anyone have a clue whether that would work or even if it would have a market???  Like I said just thinking aloud ........             Ian,  &nbsp;Interesting idea - from a technical perspective I think it could work as follows:  1. Mount the MC75 in a vehicle bracket so that the integrated camera is pointing forward through the windscreen  2. Mount a wireless IP camera at the rear similar to the one at http://www.overclock.co.uk/product/Edimax-IC-1500Wg-Wireless-11bg-Motio… connect it peer to peer to the MC75  3. The MC75 would use a small application to either capture a still shot every few seconds or record video to the SD card (front view) and to receive and store the video/still images from the IP camera (rear view). Processor load may be too high if trying to capture front and rear video at the same time so one may have to be a series of still images.  &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; Should be quite straightforward with the right camera and would not be too expensive to implement.  Best Regards, Ian Hatton EMb EMEA
