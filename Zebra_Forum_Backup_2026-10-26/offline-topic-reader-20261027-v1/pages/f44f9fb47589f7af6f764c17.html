            Hi Alberto,   &nbsp;Try the attached files which use AEKMap ( http://aeinc.ru/index.php) which is now freeware to implement a remap of the red and green keys to call executables (Pword and the Fusion menu in the sample). To test, copy the files to \Application and cold boot. To reconfigure the executable files which will be launched, edit Simple Sample.AEK with a text editor and change the executable filenames/paths.   Best Regards,  Ian Hatton
