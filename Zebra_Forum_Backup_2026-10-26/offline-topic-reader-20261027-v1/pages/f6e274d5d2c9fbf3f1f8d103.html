            Flight mode means that the radio has turned off.&nbsp; The normal ways that this can happen is if the user specifically sets the radio off.&nbsp; Or, if an application calls an API to do it.  Other abnormal conditions could cause this to happen as well.&nbsp; The network connection could become overloaded and the RIL would turn off the radio.&nbsp; If, upon resume, the CPU pegs at 100% for an inordinate amount of time due to something in the application, the radio and RIL will not be able to sync-up and the modem with turn off.&nbsp; I'm sure that there are other ways that this could happen, but I can't think of any others right now.
