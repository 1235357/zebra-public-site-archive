            Hi Wayne,
An alternative is to utilize DataWedge, which is pre-installed on the device.  You would create a DataWedge profile, associate the profile to your app, and configure how the data is input, processed, and output.  Instructions are available here: https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/latest/guide/createprofile/
There is a built-in DWDemo app that can be used to test how to capture data.  A walk-through is provided here: https://techdocs.zebra.com/datawedge/latest/guide/samples/dwdemo/
Regards,
Cathy
