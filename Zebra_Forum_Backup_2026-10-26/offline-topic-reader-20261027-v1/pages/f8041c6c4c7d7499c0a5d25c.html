                  All,   The folks at Portsmith that make a Single Slot Ethernet Cradle for the MC9000/MC9100 need our feedback on the business case for refreshing this cradle.  The current cradle (see http://www.portsmith.com/cradles/ethernet-cradle-ps6smc9000ue/ ) does not offer spare battery charging or provide a USB port for connecting to a PC or other accessories.  They would like to know if we are aware of any demand or use cases where a customer needs an MC9190 Single Slot Ethernet Cradle, but would also like to charge a spare battery and/or connect to a PC or other device via USB.    Please let me know and I'll provide this feedback to Portsmith.
