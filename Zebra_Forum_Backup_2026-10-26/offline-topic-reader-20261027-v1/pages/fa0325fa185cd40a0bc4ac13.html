            Folks,
Vitaliy from the Voice Team gave me an easier way to get PTT Express to work on your TC75
On your TC75, go to the File Browser and navigate to: /enterprise/device/settings/ptt and once you are there touch and hold the "ptt_settings..." icon until options appear.&nbsp; Tap "Delete" and then "Yes" and then go to the PTT Express icon in APPS and PTT Express should now be enabled.
Hope this helps.
