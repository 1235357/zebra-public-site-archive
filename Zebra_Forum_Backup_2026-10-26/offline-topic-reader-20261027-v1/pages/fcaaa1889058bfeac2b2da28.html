            You can use the KeyMappingMgr MX CSP to control key mappings on the Zebra Android devices and SOTI MobiControl does support the application of MX based XML files. With that said, if you have the TC75 and not the TC75x then programmatic key-mapping is not supported. More info here:
KeyMapping Manager - Zebra Technologies Techdocs
