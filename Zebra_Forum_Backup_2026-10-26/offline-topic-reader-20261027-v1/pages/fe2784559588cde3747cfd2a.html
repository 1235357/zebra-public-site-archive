                  We are experiencing an issue with label counter verification on Zebra GK420d and GK421d printers when using SGD command "odometer.total_label_count". PROBLEM:When printing multiple labels (e.g., 10 labels in one ZPL file), the odometer counter does not update immediately after printing completes. EXAMPLE (from production logs):- Sent: 10 labels (ZPL file with 10× ^XA...^XZ sequences)- Before count: 59894- After 8 seconds: 59897 (counter +3 instead of +10)- After ~22 minutes: 59904 (counter +10 - finally correct) QUESTION:1. Is this asynchronous update behavior documented anywhere?2. Is there a way to force immediate odometer update or "flush" the counter?3. Is there an alternative SGD command for real-time label counting?4. What is the recommended method for print job verification in production? CURRENT WORKAROUND:We implemented retrospective verification (checking counter on next print job),but we would prefer immediate verification for quality control. PRINTER DETAILS:- Models: GK420d, GK421d- Connection: TCP/IP RAW (port 9100)- Firmware: [můžete doplnit verzi]- Commands used: ! U1 getvar "odometer.total_label_count", ~HS Thank you for your assistance.

            Hi Alex,
In GK Series, to reduce the CPU loading and to reduce the frequent flash read/write cycles, it is implemented as delayed saving. Hence this behaviour is expected. We do not have option or way to update the odometer immediately.
