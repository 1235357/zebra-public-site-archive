            The integrated RFID capability on the TC51 is NFC only and you can implement NFC tag reading using the Xamarin component detailed at  Android.Nfc - Xamarin 
We have no support for NFC in the EMDK for Xamarin currently so you will need to use generic API's such as the one suggested above.
