                                        Overview

This guide provides important information about upgrading Zebra devices to Android 14, categorized by device platform. While this process may require careful attention, we are committed to providing guidance to ensure a smooth and efficient transition.


   For a list of device models based on Zebra platforms, refer to Zebra devices by platform.






QC4490 Upgrade Path

Migrating Zebra QC4490-platform devices to Android 14 (or higher) from earlier Android versions requires multiple upgrade steps. 

From Android 13

For QC4490 devices running Android 13, all devices must be updated to the December 2024 LifeGuard Update (or later) before upgrading to Android 14 or higher. Follow these steps:


Upgrade to the December 2024 LifeGuard Update (NE_FULL_UPDATE_13-24-08.00-TG-U00-STD-NEM-04.zip 2024) or later.

Upgrade to any Android 14 (or higher) LifeGuard Update version.


 Note: After upgrading to LifeGuard Update released in December 2024 or later, downgrading to an older version results in loss of user data and installed applications. Refer to the QC4490 OS Update instructions for more information.



QC6490 Upgrade Path

Migrating Zebra QC6490-platform devices to Android 14 from earlier Android versions requires multiple upgrade steps. 

From Android 11

For QC6490 devices running Android 11, all devices must be updated to the September 2024 LifeGuard Update (or later) before upgrading to Android 14. Follow these steps:


Upgrade to the September 2024 LifeGuard Update (AT_FULL_UPDATE_11-38-20.00-RG-U00-STD-ATH-04) or later version.

Upgrade to any Android 14 LifeGuard Update version.


 Note: After upgrading to LifeGuard Update released in September 2024 or later, downgrading to an older version results in loss of user data and installed applications. Refer to the QC6490 OS Update instructions for more information.

From Android 13

For QC6490 devices running Android 13, all devices must be updated to the March 2025 LifeGuard Update or later before upgrading to Android 14. Follow these steps:


Upgrade to the March 2025 LifeGuard Update (AT_FULL_UPDATE_13-39-18.00-TG-U00-STD-ATH-04 2025) or later version.

Upgrade to any Android 14 LifeGuard Update version.


 Note: After upgrading to LifeGuard Update released in March 2025 or later, downgrading to an older version results in loss of user data and installed applications. Refer to the QC6490 OS Update instructions for more information.



SM6375 Upgrade Path

Migrating Zebra SM6375-platform devices to Android 14 from earlier Android versions requires multiple upgrade steps. 

From Android 11

For SM6375 devices running Android 11, all devices must be updated to the October 2024 LifeGuard Update or later before upgrading to Android 14. The minimum required October LifeGuard Update versions are:


ET40, ET45: GO_FULL_UPDATE_11-38-25.00-RG-U00-STD-GSE-04 

TC15, TN28: GR_FULL_UPDATE_11-38-25.00-RG-U00-STD-GRT-04 


Follow these steps to perform the update:


Upgrade to the October 2024 LifeGuard Update or later based on your device model.

Upgrade to any Android 14 LifeGuard Update version.


 Note: After upgrading to LifeGuard Update released in October 2024 or later, downgrading to an older version results in loss of user data and installed applications. Refer to the SM6375 OS Update instructions for more information.

From Android 13

For SM6375 devices running Android 13, all devices must be updated to the September 2024 LifeGuard Update or later before upgrading to Android 14. The minimum required September LifeGuard Update versions are:


ET40, ET45: GO_FULL_UPDATE_13-33-14.00-TG-U00-STD-GSE-04 

TC15, TN28: GR_FULL_UPDATE_13-33-14.00-TG-U00-STD-GRT-04 


Follow these steps to perform the update:


Upgrade to the September 2024 LifeGuard Update or later based on your device model.

Upgrade to any Android 14 LifeGuard Update version.


 Note: After upgrading to LifeGuard Update released in September 2024 or later, downgrading to an older version results in loss of user data and installed applications. Refer to the SM6375 OS Update instructions for more information.



SD660 Upgrade Path

For instructions on migrating Zebra SD660-platform devices to Android 14 from earlier Android versions, refer to the Android 13+ Upgrade (SD660) Guide. 

Upgrade Limitations (Android 11 or earlier)

The SD660 platform does not support upgrades from Android 11 or earlier to any Android 14 LifeGuard builds through January 2026.

Migration Solutions

To migrate SD660 devices from Android 11 or earlier to Android 14, choose one of the following:


Staged Upgrade: First, upgrade the device to Android 13, then proceed with the upgrade to Android 14.

Direct Upgrade: Perform a direct upgrade using the Android 14 February 2026 LifeGuard Update or later.
