                                        Overview

Device Guardian is a part of the Zebra Guardian portfolio, a suite of solutions for device management and accountability. The Zebra Guardian portfolio includes the following components:


Device Guardian (DG) - A cloud-based solution designed to simplify the tracking and recovery of missing devices, mitigating inventory shrinkage. It leverages existing Wi-Fi network infrastructure, Bluetooth, and audio capabilities, eliminating the need for additional hardware. Device Guardian identifies the general vicinity of a missing device based on its connection to an Access Point (AP) within the facility. It also features a visual proximity indicator that provides real-time feedback on the device's location relative to the searcher using Bluetooth beacon transmissions. Additionally, if the device out of sight, an alert can be remotely activated to further pinpoint its exact location.

Device Guardian Access Management (DGAM) - An extension of Device Guardian, DGAM provides advanced features to optimize operations through local device visibility. A physical kiosk display provides real=time insights into devices within its vicinity, enabling efficient monitoring of device availability, tracking device-user assignments, ensuring devices are fully charged before use, and facilitating the swift recovery of missing devices.

Identity Guardian - Seamlessly integrates with Device Guardian to enable accountability management for both devices and users. Identity Guardian securely transmits accountability data to the cloud, offering visibility into device assignments and user responsibility. This integration enhances transparency and traceability. Note: A separate installation is required for Identity Guardian.

Guardian Cabinets - A secure, scalable solution for storing, charging, and managing the check-out and check-in of Zebra mobile devices. Guardian Cabinets can be customized with racks and Zebra cradles to fit organizational needs.


This guide focuses on Device Guardian (DG) and Device Guardian Access Management (DGAM). All sections apply to both solutions, unless otherwise specified.


  Learn about Getting Started with DG and DGAM.






Main Features

Main features of Device Guardian:


Cloud-based scalable solution hosted and maintained by Zebra with support for up to 100K devices in multiple locations.

Support for central administrator, site manager, and associate roles.

The web portal provides a corporate-level dashboard for administrators and site-level dashboard for managers to monitor and track devices.

Registration of:



Sites and access points with friendly names to easily identify device location.

Devices to assigned site with user friendly names for simple device identification.


Simple to deploy – install and configure the mobile application using StageNow and Enterprise Mobility Management tools.

Easily find a missing device with the connected AP location, the Bluetooth-based visual proximity indicator and play sound feature.

Device Beaconing Modes:



Primary BLE - This standard feature, available on all devices, uses the integrated Bluetooth radio to emit Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons when the device is enrolled with the Device Guardian server and marked as lost, provided it is not powered off.

Secondary BLE - An optional feature for select devices equipped with a secondary BLE option, whether device-based or battery-based. This enables the secondary BLE beacon to activate when the host device is powered off, allowing for tracking even with a ‘dead’ battery for a limited time.


Single Sign On (SSO) support for users to be authenticated with their SSO credentials for device access and for administrators and managers to gain access to their respective dashboards.

Generate reports to monitor device performance, user activity and site activity.

Automate workflows to ease the process of finding and tracking devices.

Automatically receive notifications from lost devices in close proximity via audio, vibrating or LED alerts.

Locate devices based on GPS coordinates and pinpoint device location on a map.

Track and locate Peripheral Devices. Optionally, Virtual Tethering warns users when a paired Bluetooth scanner approaches the edge of the Bluetooth range from its connected host device.

With Device Guardian Access Management, device management is extended to offer localized visibility into device status, enabling the optimization of operations. A kiosk display delivers insights by monitoring device availability, tracing device-user assignments, and ensuring that devices are fully charged and ready for use. Additionally, it streamlines workflows and facilitates the rapid recovery of misplaced devices through user identification and status tracking.




Important Notes


When a Zebra Android 13 or Android 11 device with file-based encryption (FBE) is secured with a PIN, password, or pattern lock, it will not connect or communicate with the server for the first time after a reboot until the user unlocks the device. As a result, the device cannot be tracked while it is locked. Once the device is unlocked after rebooting, tracking resumes and the device operates normally, even when it's relocked with a PIN, password, or pattern. This issue arises due to the Firebase Authentication API used by the client, which does not activate until the device is unlocked by the user. To ensure the device functions properly after a reboot, the device can be configured with either a swipe lock or without any lock. This allows the app to continue operating after the device reboots.

In rare cases, the Device Guardian web portal may not logout automatically after a period of inactivity. If this occurs, performing any action in the web portal results in unauthorized access error 401. The user must re-login to recover.

Starting with the April 2026 LifeGuard Update, both Google Play updates and ADB sideloading are disabled by default to ensure more reliable device staging.




New in 2.8


Group Permissions for Managers enables administrators to define custom permission groups and allocate specific web portal privileges exclusively to Manager accounts while strictly enforcing site-level access boundaries. (Applies to DGAM only.)

Cradle Lock Configuration: Configurable option for flashing an unlock sequence during checkout from locking cradles. Devices must be removed quickly to prevent automatic relocking. This setting can be disabled for cradles without physical locks.

Updated the following Reports:



Enhanced the Search field in the Devices Report to include specific filters, such as Serial Number, Device Name, etc.

Added the Device Name column to the Device Status Historical Report to facilitate easy device identification by friendly name.

Added the Serial Number column to the User Devices Historical Report.


New Peripheral Devices Tracking: Replaces the Bluetooth Scanners feature to expand Bluetooth peripherals tracking to include Bluetooth headsets in addition to Bluetooth scanners. See Requirements for supported peripheral devices.

Enhanced Kiosk Removal Process: Provided a new option to automatically reassign associated devices to a replacement kiosk before removing the original kiosk. 

New Dashboard View Configurations: Default View and Site View dashboard configurations allow administrators to customize how device metrics are aggregated and filtered. Applies to DGAM only.

Enhanced Email Notification for Missing Devices Report: The Missing (devices) CSV report now includes a "Last Status Update" column that displays the date and time of the data collection.

New Device Support: Added support for WS101, WS101-H, TC53, TC201, HC201, HC501 running Android 15 or later. For a complete list of compatible devices, see the Zebra Support Portal.

Resolved Issue: Fixed a Kiosk report issue where the Yesterday filter incorrectly returned a "No results" error.


Known Issues


Under Settings > User Management > Device User within the web portal, pagination is not working for users logged in as managers.

When a device is marked as “Missing” due to a disconnection state and then reconnects, the state updates correctly, but the missing reason remains as “Disconnected.”

On WS50 or WT6300 devices, the Missing Device notification does not navigate directly to the Missing Devices list. Instead, it opens a menu screen, from which users must select "Missing Devices" to proceed.

To successfully create or bulk upload new device users, the assigned site name length should not be more than 25 characters. Using a site name longer than 25 characters prevents users from being created.

In the Email Notification feature, when editing the email configuration, the “Status Reason” and “Select the Type of Report” options reset.

When devices are recommissioned after being decommissioned, they are not automatically reassigned to the correct site, even if site assignments are configured based on IP address.

When access points are deleted from the web console, the Device dashboard on the Android client still displays the old friendly name for the connected access points.

When the WT63 device goes offline, it fails to display a popup message notifying the user, potentially misleading them into believing the device can still be tracked while offline.

The “Modified By” column in the device user’s section displays the tenant level email address rather than the specific email ID of the user who has made the change.

Updating Missing Device Notifications settings erroneously re-triggers duplicate alert notifications to devices already in the To Be Found state. Alerts should trigger strictly upon initial transition into the To Be Found state, not during configuration updates.

Proximity Meter Freezes During Rapid Navigation: Fast switching between the Finding screen and the Mobile Device/Peripheral screens can trigger Android OS beacon scan throttling, leaving the proximity meter inoperable. To resolve this, restart the Device Guardian application.









Version History

New in 2.7


Missing Device Notifications are now fully configurable. Similar to Lost Device Nearby alerts, device notifications can be tailored using combinations of audio, vibration, LED, and customized alert messages to improve device recovery and minimize operational disruptions.

Enhanced Security for Bluetooth Proximity: Added an idle timeout alarm that triggers if a device is removed from its cradle and left unauthenticated to prevent unauthorized device removal.

No Kiosk Detected Warning: A warning notification is now displayed on the device for the following scenarios:



When Device Auto Assignment to the nearest kiosk is enabled and the device fails to detect a nearby kiosk.

When either Return Device to Same Kiosk or Return Device to Different Kiosk is enabled and the device is unable to detect a kiosk within the 90-second timeout.


Feature Relocations: Reorganized the web portal layout for better usability:



Configuration Page Removed: The One Device Per User setting is now located under User Management, and Device Auto Assignment is now located under Device Enrollment.

Bluetooth Scanner Settings: Relocated the Bluetooth Scanners notifications page to Settings > Notifications > Bluetooth Scanners (previously located directly under Settings > Bluetooth Scanners).


User Interface (UI) Updates for more consistent user experience:



Modernized Web Portal UI: Refreshed the visual design and layout across key pages: Single Sign-On Integration, Device Enrollment, Manage Users, Manage Sites, Manage Access Points, Manage Devices, Managed Kiosk Devices, Automation, Notifications (including Bluetooth Scanner), Map-Based Location, and Dashboard Reports (including Device Reports and Events by Site).

Improved Navigation: Added page-number pagination, enabling direct page navigation in data tables.

Site Page Enhancements:

Renamed the Create Site button to Add Site within the Managed Sites page for consistent terminology. When adding a site, Address, City, and Country are no longer mandatory.

Town is no longer displayed as a table column in the Site page.

The Bulk Upload .CSV file now includes the following fields: City, Country, Mobile No, Contact First Name, and Contact Last Name.

Enhanced Device Reports: The Devices Report now includes "Available Devices" and "In Use Devices," and the Status Reason column has been moved from "Decommissioned Devices" to the "Missing Devices" page.


Optimized Bulk Deletion: Removed the bulk delete operation via .CSV file. Bulk deletion is now seamlessly integrated directly into each respective category page in the web portal. To delete, select one or more rows and click Delete:



Bulk Delete Sites

Bulk Delete Access Points

Bulk Delete Devices


Added note regarding constraints on start and end IP address range entries.

Resolved Issues:



Fixed an issue where a mobile device's status persistently displayed as "Missing" on the kiosk interface, even when the device was actively in in-use or charging. The kiosk interface now updates and reflects the correct device state.

Resolved an issue where docked devices in the charging cradle continuously scanned for kiosks in an infinite loop, causing false alarms. Devices placed in a charging cradle will now scan for nearby kiosks for a maximum of 90 seconds across all Bluetooth-dependent features to prevent false alarms.

Resolved an issue where exporting a device list from the Kiosk Device page and immediately re-uploading the same file for bulk upload resulted in a false "The file is empty" error.

Resolved an issue where disabling the Lost Device Notification feature in the web console incorrectly made the Scan QR Code option unavailable. The Scan QR Code option is now displayed by default on WS30 and WT63 devices.



New in 2.6


New "Download Template" feature for Bulk Uploads: A template can now be downloaded directly from the bulk upload screen in the web portal for Kiosk, Kiosk Device, and Site configurations. This eliminates the need to export existing data or retrieve a template externally.

Added "Save Filter" and "Clear Filter" options to the DGAM Dashboard: This allows admins to lock in custom Site and Kiosk views without reapplying them in each session.

For DGAM Only:



Automated DataWedge Profile Creation: Upon installation, a DataWedge profile named "DGAM" is automatically generated to enable barcode scanning for device registration and recovery. This eliminates the need to manually create the profile.

Enhanced Feature - Device Auto Assignment: The Device Auto Assignment feature now operates concurrently with either Bluetooth Proximity or the Device Return Rule. Note: Bluetooth Proximity and the Device Return Rule remain mutually exclusive and cannot be enabled simultaneously.

New Summary Report Email Notification: The Email Notification feature has been enhanced with a "Type of Report" option, allowing administrators to choose between a "Missing" device report or a comprehensive "End of Day Device Status Report" that provides a unified view of all Available, In-Use, and Missing devices.


Resolved Issue: During the recovery process, returning the missing device to a charging cradle now automatically transitions its status from "Missing" to "Available.”


New in 2.6.1


Resolved Issues:



Fixed an intermittent tracking issue where powered-off, lost devices would randomly disappear from the "Nearby" view. System scanning and status-sync logic have been optimized to ensure beaconing devices remain consistently visible until they are recovered.

Resolved an issue, where attempting to locate an offline device from the "Device Details" page incorrectly triggered a "Never Connected" error. The application now properly recognizes the difference between a temporarily offline device and one that has never registered, enabling seamless searches for disconnected devices.

Resolved a startup crash and freeze (ANR) that occurred when launching Device Guardian for the first time immediately after configuration. Startup processes have been safely offloaded to the background to ensure a smooth, uninterrupted setup experience.



New in 2.5.1


Added support for Okta as a Single Sign-On (SSO) provider.

New Missing Device Notifications Setting: Administrators can now manage on-device alerts when a device is flagged "To Be Found" (Missing). Previously, this was enabled by default with no configuration settings.

TC501 Secondary BLE Support: Added support for Secondary Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) on the TC501 devices running BSP version ER-FULL-UPDATE-15-16-15-04-VG-U00-STD-ERS-04 or later.

Resolved Issue: Resolved an issue where the Device Guardian app became unresponsive if Bluetooth failed to enable during application launch. The app now handles this gracefully and displays the following message: “Unable to turn on Bluetooth. Please contact your administrator.”


New in 2.4


The new automated proximity verification feature enhances security by triggering a "Missing" state and an audible alarm if a device is docked in a cradle outside the vicinity of an authorized kiosk. For this feature to function, either the "Return Device to Same Kiosk" or "Return Device to Different Kiosk" option must be enabled.

Proactive SSO Certificate Management: Administrators can now directly manage Microsoft Entra ID TLS certificates through new Cert File and Key File upload fields in the SSO configuration. This capability prevents service disruptions and ensures uninterrupted access to the DG/DGAM web console without requiring external support intervention.

Updated the list of authorized domains in the Network Requirements section.

New Device Support: Added support for the TC101 device (Android 15 or higher) and expanded WS301 support to include DGAM (Android 14 or higher), which was previously supported for DG only. For a complete list of compatible devices, see the Zebra Support Portal.

Improved Historical Reports Device Tables: Device tables under Historical Reports have been updated for improved clarity.



For Cabinet Devices and User Metrics, the first three columns are now: Serial Number, Model, and Device Name for easier identification.

To simplify identification:

The Device Name and Kiosk Name columns now display their defined aliases.

The Serial Number is now located in its own separate column.


Added a "Last Updated" column in the DGAM Dashboard and Kiosk Dashboard for the Available, In use, and Missing device categories to indicate when a device transitioned into its current state.

Resolved Issues:



(SPR-60026) The device auto-assignment feature was disabled after the 2.0.1 dashboard update. This functionality is now fully restored.

(Case#25218586) Addressed a critical issue where devices charging outside the kiosk's proximity were marked as “Available.” The system now accurately prioritizes proximity - devices located away from the site’s kiosk will now enter a “Missing” state and trigger an alarm. Additionally, the "Return to Same Kiosk" warning screen now displays configured kiosk name.

The “Last Updated” column has been restored to the kiosk views (Available, In-Use, Missing) and now properly indicates the last time the device’s status changed.

Fixed an issue that caused users to be logged out of Device Guardian when unlocking their device via face recognition after an initial QR login with passcode through Identity Guardian. Users now no longer need to re-scan their QR code or manually log back in to Device Guardian following a face unlock event through Identity Guardian.



New in 2.3


New Device Support: Added support for the WS301 (DG only), TC8300, and EM45 devices with Android 14 or higher. For a complete list of compatible devices, see the Zebra Support Portal.

Focused Site Filter: The site filter on the DGAM Welcome Dashboard now provides a more focused view by restricting the display to a single site at a time. This provides a more focused view by removing the option to display multiple or all sites at once.


Improved Device Tables: The device tables are reorganized for better clarity. These changes are visible on screens such as Kiosk Devices, Reports > Devices, and the Available, In-Use, and Missing tiles on the DGAM Dashboard.



The first three columns are now: Serial Number, Model, and Device Name for easier identification.

The Device Name column now displays the defined Alias for easier identification.


Enhanced Kiosk Identification with "DisplayName": To make Kiosks easier to identify, they are now shown with their assigned "DisplayName." If a "DisplayName" is not assigned, the system defaults to showing the "DeviceModel_SerialNumber." This feature enhancement includes the following updates



Updated in UI and Reporting: The "DisplayName" is now used to identify kiosks in the following locations for at-a-glance identification: - A new "Display Name" column on the Kiosk screen - Welcome Dashboard - Kiosk Device screen - Reports > Devices page

Updated Configuration Label: The setting for identifying a kiosk in Managed Configuration was renamed from "KioskName" to "KioskName or Kiosk’s DisplayName" to reflect this new capability.


Dedicated Bulk Action Buttons: The Kiosk and Kiosk Device screens now feature dedicated Bulk Upload and Export Data buttons.

Full Individual Device User Management: Create, update, and delete individual device users directly from the portal.

Bulk Device User Deletion: Added the ability to delete multiple device users at once.

Expanded Device User Bulk Upload Limit: Increased bulk upload limit from 1,000 to 5,000 records in a single import.

Direct Kiosk Deletion: Delete kiosks directly from the web portal.

Bulk Creation of Web Portal Users: Administrators can now add multiple Admin and Manager accounts at once using a .csv file upload, significantly streamlining the user setup process.

Removed the "Delete All Records" option from the device deletion confirmation dialog.

Updated DNS Allowlist Requirements: New required DNS entries added to Network Requirements:



If wildcards are supported:

*.swengagement.zebra.com

If wildcards are not supported:

content.swengagement.zebra.com

data.swengagement.zebra.com



New in 2.2


New Device Support: Added support for the ET401 and TC501 devices with Android 15 or higher; for a complete list of compatible devices, see the Zebra Support Portal. Note: Secondary BLE support for the TC501 device is not available in this release.

New Enrollment Methods: Two additional enrollment methods have been introduced to provide more flexibility during setup. The necessary files for these methods are added to the Download Kit and are incorporated into the main setup instructions based on EMM.



XML-based Enrollment: For mass deployment of devices using an EMM.

Barcode-based Enrollment: For enrolling individual devices using Zebra's StageNow tool.


Missing Device Alert: This new feature automatically notifies all users at a site when a device is marked "To Be Found," providing a low-power method to increase the chances of a quick recovery.

Documentation Updates:



Consolidated Content: To simplify access and information flow, documentation for DG and DGAM has been unified into one comprehensive guide. All content is now applicable to both products unless specifically differentiated, and the standalone Access Management section has been retired.

New Admin Guide: Replacing the Quick Start Guide, this new resource provides administrators with comprehensive information for DG and DGAM deployment and management, covering everything from software installation to ongoing system operations.


Resolved Issues:



Corrected a user interface issue that truncated the 'checked-out' username for the last item in the Device Details list. The full username is now always visible.

Resolved a performance issue where the Welcome Dashboard could become unresponsive after navigating from the Mobile Devices page with a large number of devices (500+). The dashboard's responsiveness is now consistently maintained.



New in 2.0


New One Device Per User feature - Ensures that each user is restricted to logging into and using only one device at a time, preventing multiple simultaneous logins across different devices.

New Bluetooth Proximity Range Tuning feature in Managed Configurations - Adjusts the RSSI threshold to control the Bluetooth range between the device and kiosk, enabling devices to automatically log out when placed in a powered cradle and trigger an alarm if moved beyond the range while still logged in.

New search field in the kiosk dashboard - Provides instant search capability and ease of access to relevant information.

New Device Details in the kiosk interface - Provides key information about a device, accessible by selecting its serial number.

Enhancements to the Kiosk Dashboard - Refinements have been made to the dashboard's column layout and device information display, enhancing accessibility and usability across all device statuses - Available, In Use, and Missing - for a more streamlined and user-friendly experience.

The Device Users feature allows associates to be added to the Device Guardian system, enabling them to log in to devices using passcodes. Alternatively, admins or managers streamline this process by adding users in bulk into Zebra DNA Cloud for seamless integration with Device Guardian.

Implemented a new method for resetting user passwords through the Edit User screen.

Updated the Device Summary in the DGAM web portal by reorganizing the column layout to improve clarity and focus. Only the most relevant columns are now displayed to prioritize important information.

DGAM now supports Automation, enabling devices to automatically update their state (e.g. "Missing") when specific conditions or thresholds are met.


New in 1.1

New Device Operational Features:


Initiate Device Recovery via Kiosk - Enables a device to start a search for a misplaced device by scanning its QR Code directly at the kiosk.

Decommissioned or Lost - Provides options to mark devices as Decommissioned or Lost within the kiosk for those categorized as "In Use" or "Missing."


New Device Management Features:


Device Auto Assignment - Automatically assigns mobile devices to the nearest charging kiosk within Bluetooth range, eliminating manual device registration.

Device Return Rule - Defines whether devices must be returned to their originating kiosk or can be returned to a different kiosk; accessible during site creation.

Device Usage Reports - Provides insights into device utilization on the kiosk, detailing the number of devices that are in use, missing, or available across various time periods.

About Kiosk Updates - Provides updates to the About Kiosk screen, showing the status of Bluetooth Proximity and Return To Different Kiosk.


New Configuration Features:


Bulk Upload - Supports simultaneous upload of multiple device data for Kiosks and Devices registered with Kiosks.

Bluetooth Proximity - Automatically logs out users when a device is cradled and activates an alarm if the device is taken beyond the kiosk's Bluetooth range without user login, deactivating when the device returns within range or the user logs in; accessible during site creation.

Bulk Upload Menu Update - Adds a menu to the Bulk Upload button for sites, offering options to Add, Modify, and Delete.


New Notification Features:


Email Notifications - Delivers email reports regarding missing devices registered to kiosks.

Bulk Upload Notifications - Displays the status of bulk uploads for kiosks and their devices, indicating whether an upload has started, succeeded, or failed.


New User Account Features:


My Profile - Allows logged-in users to update their information, including changing their password; accessible from the dropdown arrow next to the user name in the web portal's title bar.
