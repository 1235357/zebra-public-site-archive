                                        Overview

The Zebra Data Provider Interface (ZDPI) is a mechanism through which device data is published by an app for consumption by other apps. ZDPI is based on the Android content provider framework, a public specification that allows sets of device data to be made available to apps using URI-based queries. 


   Only Zebra apps are authorized to publish data through OEMinfo at this time.
