                                        GET_PROFILES_LIST

Gets a list of DataWedge Profiles on the device.

Function Prototype

Intent i = new Intent();
i.setAction("com.symbol.datawedge.api.ACTION");
i.putExtra("com.symbol.datawedge.api.GET_PROFILES_LIST", "");
