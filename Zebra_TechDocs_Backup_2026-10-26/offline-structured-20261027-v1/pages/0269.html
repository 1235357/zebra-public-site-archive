About Zebra Tread Intel

Zebra Tread Intel 1.0

Overview

Zebra Tread Intel captures tread depth information for tires. It is a snap-on accessory that connects to a compatible Zebra mobile computer, functioning as a USB host. Users swipe the reader across the tire's surface to detect and record data while the tire is mounted on a wheel.

This document provides guidance for developers on accessing Zebra Tread Intel functionality through their applications. It details the methods needed to retrieve tread data from the reader accessory.

Requirements

  • Android 11 or higher
  • See the Zebra Support Portal for the supported devices list
  • Accessory Tire Zebra Tread Intel Snap-On attached to the supported mobile computer. See the Support Portal for compatible mobile computers.
  • Download the Zebra Tread Intel package, which includes the libraries necessary to use the Tread Intel APIs.
